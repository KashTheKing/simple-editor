Timeline

The Timeline is where your edit lives: video tracks (V1, V2…) on top, audio tracks (A1, A2…) below, time running left to right, and the red playhead marking the frame the Preview shows.

The toolbar​

One row above the tracks:

  • Tools: Select (V), Blade (C) and Rate stretch (R).
  • Snapping (the magnet, S).
  • On the right: zoom out, the zoom slider, zoom in, and Zoom to Fit (Shift+Z).

Scroll to move up and down, Shift+scroll to move sideways, Ctrl+scroll to zoom, and drag with the middle mouse button to pan. Click or drag on the ruler to move the playhead.

Select​

  • Click a clip to select it. Its linked audio or video comes with it; Alt+click takes just the one clip.
  • Ctrl+click adds or removes a clip, Shift+click adds one.
  • Drag across empty track space to select everything in the box (Shift+drag adds to the selection).
  • Click an empty gap between clips to select the gap; Delete then closes it.
  • Ctrl+A selects everything, A / Shift+A everything after / before the playhead.
  • Link / Unlink (Ctrl+L) ties clips together or splits a video from its sound.

Cut​

  • Split at Playhead (Ctrl+B) cuts the selected clips at the playhead. With nothing selected it cuts everything under the playhead.
  • The Blade tool (C): click a clip to cut there. It cuts every track at that time; a thin line shows where while you hover.

To remove clips:

PressWhat happens
Delete (or Backspace)Removes the clip and leaves a gap
Shift+DeleteRipple Delete: removes it and closes the gap, pulling everything after it left
; with In and Out markedLift: removes that time range from every track, leaving a gap
' with In and Out markedExtract: removes the range and closes the gap

Right-click a gap ▸ Close Gap, or select it and press Delete, to close a gap you left.

Move​

Drag a clip to move it. It won't land on top of another clip (if it would overlap, it stays where it was). Drag it above the top video track or below the bottom audio track to create a new track.

Trim​

Drag the edge of a clip to make it shorter or longer. Hold a key while you drag for the pro versions of the same move:

HoldDrag a clip's bodyDrag a clip's edge
nothingMoveTrim (leaves a gap, or eats into one)
CtrlMoveRipple trim: later clips follow, so no gap opens
AltSlip: the content slides inside the clip, the clip stays putRoll: moves the cut between two clips, both change length
ShiftMoveRate stretch: changes the clip's speed so it fits the new length
Ctrl+AltSlide: moves the clip, its neighbours shrink or grow to make roomTrims the same edge of every selected clip by the same amount
Ctrl+ShiftInsert-move: lifts the clip out (closing its gap) and pushes it in where you drop

When you drag something in from the Library, the keys mean: nothing = place it on a free track, Ctrl = insert (later clips move right), Alt = overwrite, Shift = on a new track on top.

A magnetic track (see tracks) closes gaps for you: a plain edge drag ripples and moving a clip pushes its neighbours. Esc during a drag cancels it. F1 lists every gesture.

Trimming with the keyboard​

  1. Put the playhead near a cut and press U (Select Nearest Edit Point). Clicking right on a cut does the same; Ctrl+click picks the outgoing side, Alt+click the incoming side.
  2. Shift+U cycles between both sides (a roll), the left side and the right side.
  3. [ / ] trim one frame, Ctrl+[ / Ctrl+] ten frames. E extends the edit to the playhead.

Also handy: Q / W trim the clip's start / end to the playhead, Alt+, / Alt+. slip it by a frame, and , / . nudge it a frame. All of these are also under Timeline ▸ Trim.

Snapping​

With snapping on (the magnet, S), whatever you drag snaps to, in this order: the playhead, the edges of the selected clips, other clip edges, markers, transitions, and the In and Out marks. A vertical guide line shows the snap point before you let go. Zoom in and the pull gets weaker, so frame-exact moves don't fight the magnet.

Tracks​

Each track's header shows its name, a lock, and an eye (video) or speaker (audio). An S for solo appears when you hover the header. Double-click the name to rename it; drag the colour stripe on the left edge to reorder tracks; drag the bottom edge of a track (or Alt+scroll over it) to make it taller.

Right-click a header for:

  • Add Video Track, Add Audio Track, Remove Track (only an empty one).
  • Hidden / Muted, and Solo.
  • Locked: nothing on the track can be moved or trimmed.
  • Ripple (Sync): this track moves along when you ripple-delete, insert or extract on another track. On for V1 and A1 by default.
  • Magnetic Track: no gaps on this track; edge drags ripple and Delete closes the gap.
  • Rename…, Colour, Move Track Up / Down.

Right-click menus​

Right-click a clip for cut, copy, paste, delete and Split at Playhead, plus grouped submenus: Speed (retime, freeze frame), Transform, Add (marker, transition, mask), Audio (bus, normalize, ducking, beats), Nest, Transcript, Label, then Match Frame, Reveal in Library and Save Selection as Template….

Other spots have their own menus: the ruler (markers, In / Out, paste, view options), a gap (Close Gap, paste, add tracks), a cut between two clips (roll, add a transition), a transition (remove, change type or easing, move it to another cut), a marker (rename, delete, label) and the subtitle lane. They're all in the right-click reference.

Sequences and nesting​

A sequence is a timeline inside your timeline, like a compound clip.

  1. Select some clips and press Alt+N (Clip ▸ Nest Selection into a New Sequence). They're replaced by one sequence clip, with its sound on an audio track.
  2. Double-click the sequence clip to edit inside it. The Timeline's tab now reads Main plus the sequence's name with an ×.
  3. Click Main or the × (or press Alt+Up) to go back.

Un-nest Sequence Clip (in the clip's right-click ▸ Nest menu) puts the clips back. New empty sequences come from the Library: right-click an empty spot ▸ New sequence. Sequences are listed in the Library; drag one onto the timeline to use it again.

Markers​

  • Press M to add a marker at the playhead. If a selected clip is under the playhead, the marker sticks to that clip; otherwise it goes on the ruler.
  • Drag a marker to move it, double-click it to jump there, right-click it to rename, delete or colour it.
  • The Markers panel (Ctrl+8) lists them all; click one to jump to it. See Advanced.

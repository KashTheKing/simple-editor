Viewer & playback

The Preview shows your edit at the playhead. It's also where you move and resize things: drag on the picture instead of typing numbers.

Play and move around​

The row under the picture has, from left to right: the timecode, the transport buttons (Go to Start, Step Back 1 Frame, Play / Pause, Step Forward 1 Frame, Go to End), the zoom menu (Fit, 50 %, 100 %, 200 %) and fullscreen. Above it, a thin scrub bar grows when you hover it; click or drag it to jump.

Click the timecode to type a time: 1:30, 00:01:30:12, +10 (frames) or -2s all work. Enter jumps there, Esc cancels.

In and Out marks show on the timeline's ruler. They're used by Lift, Extract, Loop, and by export if you tick Export In/Out Range.

Ctrl+scroll zooms the picture, dragging with the middle mouse button pans it, and Fit resets.

The tool rail​

The column of buttons at the top-left of the picture picks what a drag on the picture does:

ToolKeyDrag on the picture to…
SelectVMove the selected clip, and use its handles (below)
CropDrag the selected clip's edges to crop it
TextTAdd a text clip there (5 seconds long, at the playhead)
ShapeShift+S cyclesDraw a rectangle, ellipse, triangle, polygon, star, line or arrow. Click the button to pick one
DrawDDraw strokes by hand; the record button draws while the video plays
MaskGDraw a mask on the selected clip: rectangle, ellipse, polygon or path

While a shape or draw tool is on, an options bar along the top sets the fill, stroke, stroke width, corner radius, number of sides or brush. Hold Shift while drawing a shape to keep its proportions (lines snap to 45°), and Alt to draw it out from the centre. For a polygon, click each corner and press Enter (or click the first point) to close it.

Move, scale, rotate and crop​

Select a clip on the timeline and it gets a box with handles in the Preview (with the Select tool):

  • Drag inside the picture to move it.
  • Drag a corner to scale it evenly; drag an edge's middle to stretch it one way.
  • Drag the knob above the top edge to rotate it. Hold Shift to snap to 15° steps.
  • Turn on Crop Handles (right-click ▸ Crop Handles, or the Crop tool) and the edge handles crop instead.

While you drag, the clip snaps to the centre, edges and thirds of the frame and to other clips, with a guide line to show it. Turn that off with right-click ▸ Snap to Canvas. For exact numbers, use the Inspector. If the clip is animated, a drag sets a keyframe at the playhead.

Right-click the picture​

Right-clicking a clip selects it. Then, for that clip: Reset Transform, Fill Frame, Stretch to Frame, Crop Handles, Add Mask to Selection and Add Text Here. Always there:

  • Stop, Mark In, Mark Out, Clear In / Out.
  • Zoom and Playback Resolution (100 % down to 25 %: lower is smoother on a slow PC).
  • Proxies: play light-weight copies of big video files. On by default; they're built in the background and never used for export.
  • Movie Mode (pre-render): render ahead so heavy effects play smoothly.
  • Social Guides: safe-area outlines for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and more.
  • Show / Hide Scopes, Background (checkerboard, black, white or a colour) and Snap to Canvas.

Multicam angles​

When the selected clip (or the clip under the playhead) is a multicam clip, an Angles list appears in the Preview's top-right corner. Click an angle to cut to it at the playhead. Make a multicam clip from two or more clips of the same moment with Clip ▸ Create Multicam from Selection; they're synced by their sound. Close the list with its ×, and bring it back with Clip ▸ Multicam Angles….

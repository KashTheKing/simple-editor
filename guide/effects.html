Effects, transitions & titles

The Effects, Transitions and Gallery panels sit next to the Library on the Edit page. Each is a catalogue of cards: pick one and apply it to the selected clips. What a clip already has is edited in the Inspector.

Effects​

  1. Select one or more clips on the timeline.
  2. Open Effects (Ctrl+3) and click a card. It's added to every selected clip.

You can also drag a card onto a clip on the timeline, or right-click a card ▸ Add to selected clip(s) / Add to all clips on this track. Type in the search box to find one.

The catalogue has blur, motion blur, pixelate, VHS, glow, vignette, sharpen, chroma key (green screen), colour replace, crop, levels, curves, hue and saturation, black & white, flip, 3D plane, camera shake, LUTs and a Custom Shader you write yourself in GLSL.

The effect stack​

A clip's effects are listed in the Inspector's Effects section, top to bottom in the order they're applied. Each row is a grip, a checkbox and the effect's name:

  • The checkbox turns an effect off without removing it.
  • Drag the grip to change the order.
  • Click the name to show or hide its settings. Every setting has a ◆ to animate it.
  • Right-click the name for Add mask (limit the effect to a shape you draw on the Preview), Remove mask, Edit shader… (Custom Shader), Copy parameters / Paste parameters (to another clip with the same effect), Move up / Move down, Open in Node editor and Remove.

Adjustment layers (Clip ▸ Add ▸ Add Adjustment Layer, Ctrl+Alt+L) are empty clips whose effects apply to everything below them on the timeline, for as long as the layer lasts.

Transitions​

A transition blends one clip into the next at a cut. There are four: Cross Fade, Fade to Color, Push and Wipe. The row above the cards sets the Duration of the next one you add (1 second to start with), plus its colour (Fade to Color) or the direction it comes From (Push, Wipe).

  • Drag a card onto a cut on the timeline. Drop on the right half of a clip for its end, the left half for its start.
  • Or select clips and right-click a card ▸ Add at start of selected clip(s), Add at end of selected clip(s) or Add at every cut on this track. At the start or end of the timeline, you get a fade from or to black.
  • Or click a cut and press Ctrl+Shift+T (Add Transition at Selected Cut) for a one-second Cross Fade. Ctrl+T adds the last one you used again. Both are in Clip ▸ Transition too.

Hovering a card previews it in the Preview, when the selected clip is under the playhead.

To change a transition, click its band on the timeline: the Inspector shows its kind, duration and easing. Drag the band's edges to make it longer, or right-click it ▸ Change Type, Change Easing, Move To (the clip's start or end, or the cut with the previous or next clip; the transition keeps its settings) or Remove Transition.

The Gallery​

The Gallery holds ready-made presets, one tab each. Click a card to apply it to the selected clips, or right-click it (Apply to selection, Apply to every caption, Place at playhead) to see it in the Preview first:

TabWhat a click does
LooksA colour grade. It replaces the clip's effects. To keep your own, select a graded clip and right-click an empty spot ▸ Save look from selection…
LutsAdds a .cube LUT from your LUT folders
CaptionsSets the style of all subtitles (Classic, Bold Outline, Boxed, Karaoke…)
SpeedGives the clip a speed ramp (Montage, Hero, Bullet, Jump, Flash)
TemplatesPlaces a clip template you saved at the playhead (Clip ▸ Save Selection as Template…)
TitlesPlaces a title (below)

Titles and text​

  • A quick text: Clip ▸ Add ▸ Add Text Clip (Shift+T) puts a 5-second text clip at the playhead. Or pick the Text tool (T) and drag on the Preview, or right-click the Preview ▸ Add Text Here.
  • A designed title: Gallery ▸ Titles has Lower Third, Title Card and Caption Box. Click one to place it; a Customize box under the cards lets you change its text and colours.
  • Type the words, pick the font and style in the Inspector, and drag the text on the Preview to place it.
  • To animate it, use the Inspector's Animation presets (Slide, Zoom, Pop, Fade, Spin), the Reveal and Wave sliders, or your own keyframes.
  • Save a style you like with the Inspector's Text style presets and apply it to other text clips.

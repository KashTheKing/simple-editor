Inspector & keyframes

The Inspector shows the settings of whatever you selected. It's in the top-right corner of every page (Ctrl+2 shows or hides it).

What it shows​

  • Nothing selected: the project: its Name, Format (YouTube, TikTok, Square 1:1… or a format you saved), Resolution, Frame rate and Background. Right-click the Project title to save the current format as a template, or delete one you saved.
  • A clip: its settings (below).
  • Several clips: the settings they share (transform, opacity, blend, fades, enabled, label). Changing one changes all of them.
  • A transition (click its band on the timeline): its kind, duration, easing, and colour or direction where they apply.

A clip's settings​

At the top is one row: the label dot (click it to pick a colour label), the clip's name (click to rename) and an eye that switches the clip off and on. Under it, one grey line says which track it's on, where it starts and how long it is.

Below that, the settings are in sections. Only the one you most likely need starts open; click any section's title to open or close it, and right-click a title for that section's extra commands.

SectionWhat's in it
Text (text clips)The text, font (Import font… adds your own), size, bold, italic, fill, outline, shadow, alignment, spacing, a background box, Reveal (typewriter) and Wave, and Animation presets
TransformPosition X / Y, Scale (click the chain to scale X and Y separately), Rotation
Opacity & blendOpacity, blend mode (Multiply, Screen, Overlay…), fade in and out
Audio (clips on an audio track)Volume, pan, fades, Role and Bus. Right-click the title for Repair, Clarity, Open in Mixer, Duck Music under Dialogue and Normalize Selection (Audio)
SpeedSpeed %, Reverse and Freeze. Right-click for Speed / Retime… (Ctrl+R) and Freeze Frame at Playhead
ColorThe grading controls (Color)
EffectsThe clip's effect stack (Effects). Right-click the title to open it in the node editor
Shape, Mask, PathA shape's fill, stroke and size; the clip's mask (right-click: Add mask, Edit in viewport, Remove mask); saving the clip's motion as a path
AssetThe file behind the clip and how often it's used

The section that starts open depends on the clip and the page: Color on the Color page, Audio for anything on an audio track, Text for text, Shape for shapes, Effects for adjustment layers, and Transform for everything else.

Drag a number left or right to change it, or click it to type a value. Dragging the clip in the Preview changes the same values.

Keyframes​

Any value with a ◆ next to it can be animated. To make something change over time:

  1. Move the playhead to where the change should start and click ◆. The value there becomes a keyframe.
  2. Move the playhead to where it should end and change the value. Once a property has a keyframe, every change adds or updates a keyframe at the playhead.
  3. Play it back. The value glides between keyframes.

The ◆ shows where you are: filled when there's a keyframe at the playhead (click it to remove that keyframe), outlined when the value is animated but not keyed right here, and faint when it isn't animated at all.

Right-click the ◆ (or the value) for:

  • Previous key / Next key: jump the playhead to this property's keyframes.
  • Clear keyframes: remove all of them, keeping the value at the playhead.
  • Link to path ▸ (Position X / Y): follow a path you saved from a shape's Path section or from motion tracking.
  • Link to expression…: drive the value with a small Luau formula, like return value + math.sin(t*4) * 20 for a wobble. t is the time in seconds from the clip's start and value is what the property would be without the expression. The formula has to end with return and a number.
  • Unlink: back to normal. Editing a linked value by hand unlinks it too. A linked value shows ∿ instead of ◆.

Alt+Left / Alt+Right jump to the previous / next keyframe of the selected clip, over all its properties.

Keyframes also show as small diamonds on the clip in the timeline. Drag them to change their timing, and right-click one for Easing (Linear, Ease In, Ease Out, Hold, Overshoot…) or Delete Keyframe. For full control over the curves, open the Curves panel (Ctrl+5), which draws every animated value of the selected clip as a graph you can edit (Advanced).

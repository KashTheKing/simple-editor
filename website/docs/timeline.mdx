![The timeline](/img/tutorial/timeline.png)

The **Timeline** is where your edit lives: video tracks (V1, V2…) on top, audio tracks (A1, A2…)
below, time running left to right, and the red **playhead** marking the frame the Preview shows.

## The toolbar

One row above the tracks:

- **Tools**: **Select** (<kbd>V</kbd>), **Blade** (<kbd>C</kbd>) and **Rate stretch** (<kbd>R</kbd>).
- **Snapping** (the magnet, <kbd>S</kbd>).
- On the right: zoom out, the zoom slider, zoom in, and **Zoom to Fit** (<kbd>Shift+Z</kbd>).

Scroll to move up and down, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+scroll to move sideways, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll to zoom,
and drag with the middle mouse button to pan. Click or drag on the ruler to move the playhead.

## Select

- **Click** a clip to select it. Its linked audio or video comes with it; <kbd>Alt</kbd>+click takes just
  the one clip.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click adds or removes a clip, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click adds one.
- **Drag across empty track space** to select everything in the box (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+drag adds to the
  selection).
- **Click an empty gap** between clips to select the gap; <kbd>Delete</kbd> then closes it.
- <kbd>Ctrl+A</kbd> selects everything, <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>Shift+A</kbd> everything after / before the
  playhead.
- **Link / Unlink** (<kbd>Ctrl+L</kbd>) ties clips together or splits a video from its sound.

## Cut

- **Split at Playhead** (<kbd>Ctrl+B</kbd>) cuts the selected clips at the playhead. With nothing
  selected it cuts everything under the playhead.
- The **Blade** tool (<kbd>C</kbd>): click a clip to cut there. It cuts every track at that time; a thin
  line shows where while you hover.

![The Blade tool](/img/tutorial/timeline-blade.png)

To remove clips:

| Press | What happens |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> (or <kbd>Backspace</kbd>) | Removes the clip and leaves a gap |
| <kbd>Shift+Delete</kbd> | **Ripple Delete**: removes it and closes the gap, pulling everything after it left |
| <kbd>;</kbd> with In and Out marked | **Lift**: removes that time range from every track, leaving a gap |
| <kbd>'</kbd> with In and Out marked | **Extract**: removes the range and closes the gap |

Right-click a gap ▸ **Close Gap**, or select it and press <kbd>Delete</kbd>, to close a gap you left.

## Move

Drag a clip to move it. It won't land on top of another clip (if it would overlap, it stays where it
was). Drag it above the top video track or below the bottom audio track to create a new track.

## Trim

Drag the **edge** of a clip to make it shorter or longer. Hold a key while you drag for the pro
versions of the same move:

| Hold | Drag a clip's **body** | Drag a clip's **edge** |
|---|---|---|
| nothing | **Move** | **Trim** (leaves a gap, or eats into one) |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> | Move | **Ripple trim**: later clips follow, so no gap opens |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> | **Slip**: the content slides inside the clip, the clip stays put | **Roll**: moves the cut between two clips, both change length |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> | Move | **Rate stretch**: changes the clip's speed so it fits the new length |
| <kbd>Ctrl+Alt</kbd> | **Slide**: moves the clip, its neighbours shrink or grow to make room | Trims the same edge of every selected clip by the same amount |
| <kbd>Ctrl+Shift</kbd> | **Insert-move**: lifts the clip out (closing its gap) and pushes it in where you drop | |

When you drag something in from the Library, the keys mean: nothing = place it on a free track,
<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> = insert (later clips move right), <kbd>Alt</kbd> = overwrite, <kbd>Shift</kbd> = on a new
track on top.

A **magnetic** track (see [tracks](#tracks)) closes gaps for you: a plain edge drag ripples and moving a
clip pushes its neighbours. <kbd>Esc</kbd> during a drag cancels it. <kbd>F1</kbd> lists every gesture.

### Trimming with the keyboard

1. Put the playhead near a cut and press <kbd>U</kbd> (**Select Nearest Edit Point**). Clicking right on
   a cut does the same; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click picks the outgoing side, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+click the incoming
   side.
2. <kbd>Shift+U</kbd> cycles between both sides (a roll), the left side and the right side.
3. <kbd>[</kbd> / <kbd>]</kbd> trim one frame, <kbd>Ctrl+[</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+]</kbd> ten frames.
   <kbd>E</kbd> extends the edit to the playhead.

Also handy: <kbd>Q</kbd> / <kbd>W</kbd> trim the clip's start / end to the playhead,
<kbd>Alt+,</kbd> / <kbd>Alt+.</kbd> slip it by a frame, and <kbd>,</kbd> / <kbd>.</kbd> nudge it a frame.
All of these are also under **Timeline ▸ Trim**.

## Snapping

With **snapping** on (the magnet, <kbd>S</kbd>), whatever you drag snaps to, in this order: the
playhead, the edges of the selected clips, other clip edges, markers, transitions, and the In and Out
marks. A vertical guide line shows the snap point **before** you let go. Zoom in and the pull gets
weaker, so frame-exact moves don't fight the magnet.

## Tracks

![Right-click a track header](/img/tutorial/timeline-track-menu.png)

Each track's header shows its name, a **lock**, and an **eye** (video) or **speaker** (audio). An **S**
for solo appears when you hover the header. Double-click the name to rename it; drag the colour stripe on
the left edge to reorder tracks; drag the bottom edge of a track (or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+scroll over it) to
make it taller.

Right-click a header for:

- **Add Video Track**, **Add Audio Track**, **Remove Track** (only an empty one).
- **Hidden** / **Muted**, and **Solo**.
- **Locked**: nothing on the track can be moved or trimmed.
- **Ripple (Sync)**: this track moves along when you ripple-delete, insert or extract on another track.
  On for V1 and A1 by default.
- **Magnetic Track**: no gaps on this track; edge drags ripple and <kbd>Delete</kbd> closes the gap.
- **Rename…**, **Colour**, **Move Track Up** / **Down**.

## Right-click menus

![Right-click a clip](/img/tutorial/timeline-clip-menu.png)

**Right-click a clip** for cut, copy, paste, delete and **Split at Playhead**, plus grouped submenus:
**Speed** (retime, freeze frame), **Transform**, **Add** (marker, transition, mask), **Audio** (bus,
normalize, ducking, beats), **Nest**, **Transcript**, **Label**, then **Match Frame**, **Reveal in
Library** and **Save Selection as Template…**.

Other spots have their own menus: the **ruler** (markers, In / Out, paste, view options), a **gap**
(Close Gap, paste, add tracks), a **cut** between two clips (roll, add a transition), a **transition**
(remove, change type or easing, move it to another cut), a **marker** (rename, delete, label) and the **subtitle lane**. They're
all in the [right-click reference](right-click.mdx#timeline).

![Right-click the ruler](/img/tutorial/timeline-ruler-menu.png)

## Sequences and nesting

A **sequence** is a timeline inside your timeline, like a compound clip.

1. Select some clips and press <kbd>Alt+N</kbd> (**Clip ▸ Nest Selection into a New Sequence**). They're
   replaced by one sequence clip, with its sound on an audio track.
2. **Double-click** the sequence clip to edit inside it. The Timeline's tab now reads **Main** plus the
   sequence's name with an ×.
3. Click **Main** or the × (or press <kbd>Alt+Up</kbd>) to go back.

![Editing inside a sequence](/img/tutorial/timeline-nested.png)

**Un-nest Sequence Clip** (in the clip's right-click ▸ **Nest** menu) puts the clips back. New empty
sequences come from the Library: right-click an empty spot ▸ **New sequence**. Sequences are listed in
the Library; drag one onto the timeline to use it again.

## Markers

- Press <kbd>M</kbd> to add a marker at the playhead. If a selected clip is under the playhead, the marker
  sticks to that clip; otherwise it goes on the ruler.
- Drag a marker to move it, double-click it to jump there, right-click it to rename, delete or colour it.
- The **Markers** panel (<kbd>Ctrl+8</kbd>) lists them all; click one to jump to it. See
  [Advanced](advanced.mdx#markers).

![Right-click a marker](/img/tutorial/timeline-marker-menu.png)

**Project Context** is where you tell the [Assistant](assistant.mdx) what the project is. It is one plain
Markdown document, saved inside the project file, with four sections:

- **About**: what the video is, who it is for, where it goes.
- **Rules**: things the assistant must always or never do ("no music with lyrics", "keep the logo in the
  top left"). Rules are sent as instructions.
- **Style guide**: how it should look and feel: pacing, captions, colour, sound.
- **Links**: references, a script, the brief.

Every Assistant request (chat, tasks and the comments it handles) starts with the whole document, so you
never have to repeat yourself. An agent connected over MCP reads it with `context.get`.

![The Project Context panel beside the Assistant](/img/tutorial/project-context.png)

## Open it

- the notepad button at the top right of the Assistant (lit while the panel is open; click it again to close it),
- **⋯ ▸ Project Context** in the Assistant,
- **Window ▸ Project Context**, or the command palette (`Ctrl+K`, "Project Context").

Type straight into the document. A new project shows the four headings to fill in. The eye button switches
between editing and a Markdown preview. Each burst of typing is one undo step.

## The digest

Under the document, **Digest** (folded away until you open it) shows what else the assistant gets, gathered
by itself and read-only:

- **Planner**: every item with its checklist and notes, ticked or not,
- **Notes**: each note's title and text,
- **Moodboard**: the moodboard's pictures with their tags, and the notes under a planner item's pictures,
- **Open comments**: timeline comments that are not ticked off, with their times.

The buttons above it open the **Planner**, **Moodboard** and **Markers** panels, where you change those.

## Editing styles

A style from [Editing Styles](editing-styles.mdx) can be applied to the project: `/style vlog` in the
Assistant (or **Apply** in its Styles tab) writes the style's guidance at the top of **Style guide** and its
measurable rules at the top of **Rules**, between two comment lines you can see:

```markdown
## Rules

<!-- style: vlog -->
- Average shot length about 1.9 s (most shots 1.14-3.04 s).
- Mix to -14 LUFS integrated.
<!-- /style -->

- Keep the logo in the top left.
```

What you wrote stays below it. Applying another style replaces it; **Remove** at the top of the panel,
`/style off`, or Undo takes it back out and leaves your own text exactly as it was.

## For agents (MCP)

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `context.get` | `{text, sections {About, Rules, Style guide, Links}, style, digest}` |
| `context.set` | `text` replaces the document, or `section` + `body` replaces one section; one undo step |
| `styles.apply` / `styles.remove` | apply a library style to the project / take it out; one undo step each |

![A frame from the trailer](/img/trailer/trailer-frame.jpg)

The 1.4.0 trailer was cut entirely in Simple Editor: its own timeline, keyframes, the **3D Plane** and **Motion Blur**
effects, an audio visualizer and plain Text clips for every title. Nothing was done in another program. This page shows
how each part was made, so you can make one like it for your own app, game or channel.

:::tip Download the project
**[Download the trailer project (.zip)](#download)** - open `Simple Editor 1.4.0 Trailer.sedit`, drop your own song
on the **Music** track and press Play. Every section has a comment that explains it.
:::

## What you need

- Screen recordings of the things you want to show, one short take each (5 to 15 seconds).
- A song with a clear beat.
- About an hour.

## 1. Find the beat first

Everything in the trailer lands on the music, so the song comes first.

1. Put the song on an audio track and open the **Beat Aligner** (the tab beside the Timeline on the Audio page).
2. Press **Detect**. The trailer's song was read as 199.99 BPM: twice its real speed. **½** turned it into **100 BPM**.
3. Move **Offset** onto a strong downbeat. Here that was 2.128 s into the song, so the clip starts there and the
   trailer's 0:00 is a downbeat.

At 100 BPM a beat is 0.6 s and a bar of four beats is **2.4 s**. The song's sections change every two bars
(4.8 s), and the band comes in on the downbeat 7 bars in. So the plan was:

| Bars | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 0:00-0:04.8 | title card over the quiet intro |
| 3-7 | 0:04.8-0:16.8 | the build: five features, one bar each |
| 8 | 0:16.8 | **the drop**: the band comes in |
| 8-21 | 0:16.8-0:50.4 | fourteen more features, one bar each |
| 22-23 | 0:50.4-0:55.2 | a wall of four screens |
| 24-25 | 0:55.2-1:00 | end card; the song ends on a phrase boundary |

A marker on every bar (the **DROP** one in red) makes the grid visible on the timeline. **Project settings ▸ Max
length** set to 1:00 draws the red end line, and the export stops there.

## 2. Record the screens

Each feature is one short take of the app in use, recorded at 1920×1080 with the
**Screen Recording** panel (Window ▸ Screen Recording) (**Application / Game capture** records one window even when
it is covered). The demo project in the takes uses footage made from scratch, so the trailer has no third-party clips.

Keep takes short and do one thing per take: click **Detect**, drag a template in, switch platforms. A take that is
a little longer than you need is fine; the next step speeds it up to fit.

The cursor in the takes was drawn on afterwards with a click ripple in the accent colour, so every click reads even
when the screen is small and moving.

## 3. One shot per bar

![The trailer project on the Edit page](/img/trailer/edit-page.jpg)

![The trailer timeline: background, screens, wall, labels, music and sound effects](/img/trailer/timeline.jpg)

Each take goes on the **Screens** track exactly on a bar line. Then its **Speed** (Inspector ▸ Transform) is set so
the part of the take you want fills the bar: a 4.4 s stretch in a 2.4 s bar plays at 183 %. Fast takes read as
energy; nobody needs to see a click at normal speed in a trailer.

Make the cuts land exactly on frames. At 60 fps a 2.4 s bar is exactly 144 frames, so every cut is clean.

## 4. The 3D move

![A shot's Position X and Scale keyframes](/img/trailer/inspector-transform.jpg)

Every screen moves the same way in four phases, and each shot comes in from the other side (right, left, right),
so the motion keeps flowing:

| When | Position X | Scale | Ease to the next key |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 s (the downbeat) | 760 px to the side | 37 % | fast start, soft landing: `cubic-bezier(0.12, 0.9, 0.22, 1)` |
| 0.3 s | 30 px | 72 % | linear drift |
| beat 3 (1.2 s) | | 74 % → 81 % → 74 % | a quick push in and back: the **punch** on the beat |
| 0.22 s before the cut | -30 px | 75 % | slow start, fast exit: `cubic-bezier(0.75, 0, 0.95, 0.45)` |
| the cut | 980 px to the other side | 41 % | |

So a screen flies in fast and lands softly on the downbeat, drifts while you read it, punches on the third beat and
whips out just before the next cut.

![The 3D Plane and Motion Blur on a shot](/img/trailer/inspector-3d-plane.jpg)

The tilt is a **3D Plane** effect (Effects ▸ Transform) with **Yaw**, **Pitch** and **Roll** keyframed on the same
four times: 42° of yaw on the way in, 13° while it drifts, -44° on the way out, with a few degrees of pitch and roll
so it never looks flat. Its **Distance** stays at 2.414, the distance at which a screen fills the frame, so the size
comes only from Scale.

**Motion Blur** (Shutter angle 300°, 16 samples) smears the fast parts of the move and leaves the slow drift sharp.
It is drawn by the graphics card, so you see it in a GPU export.

Shape the eases on the Animate page's **Curves** tab: a steep start and a long flat landing for the fly-in, the
opposite for the whip out.

## 5. The background

The dark grid of dots behind the screens is the **Spectrum Bars** audio visualizer: Gallery ▸ Audio ▸ Visualizers,
dropped on the bottom video track over the song. It reacts to the music by itself, so the background moves on every
beat without a single keyframe.

## 6. Labels and titles

Every feature gets a pill at the top left: a small tag (NEW or 1.4), the name and one short line under it. Each
pill is two ordinary **Text** clips:

| Clip | Style |
|---|---|
| `NEW  \|  BEAT ALIGNER` | Montserrat (Black), 58 px, white, a **Box** behind it in the area's colour with 18 px padding, a soft shadow |
| the line under it | Montserrat, 30 px, white, a darker shadow so it reads over any screen |

Both slide in from the left on the downbeat and out just before the next cut. That is one keyframed property,
**Position X**: 900 px off to the left, then home in 0.32 s with the same fast-start ease as the screens, then away
to the left in the last 0.3 s with the whip ease. A quick **Opacity** fade hides the ends. The line under the title
starts 0.08 s later, so the two arrive one after the other.

The colour says what part of the app the feature is in: blue for editing, green for audio, amber for export, pink
for templates, cyan for the emulator, purple for animation and the Assistant, orange for colour, red for recording.

The title and end cards are three Text clips each: the name at 150 px, the version in a pink box half a beat later,
and a spaced-out line a beat later. Their **Scale** goes from 25 % to 100 % with the fast-start ease, and on the
last bar line up to 240 % while the **Opacity** drops to 0, so the card blows out towards you.

## 7. The screen wall

![The screen wall](/img/trailer/trailer-wall.jpg)

Two bars before the end card, four takes play at once in a 2×2 grid, moved ±470 px across and ±265 px up and
down. Each scales in to 45 % half a beat after the one before, so they land one after another, and each has its own
3D Plane tilt. Select the four clips and **Nest** them (Ctrl+P, type Nest), and the wall becomes one clip on one track.

## 8. Sound design

![History: one labelled step per technique](/img/trailer/history.jpg)

- A **whoosh on every cut**, placed so its loudest moment is on the cut, not its start. Four different whooshes
  take turns so it never sounds like a loop.
- A **riser** that peaks on the drop, and an **impact** on it.
- A second impact under the end card.

All sound effects are from Mixkit (free licence, no credit needed). The whooshes sit at about half volume under the
music.

## 9. Comments and history

Every part of the project has a comment marker that says how it was made: open the **Markers** panel or hover a
marker. Each technique was also done as one labelled undo step, so the **History** panel reads like a recipe
("Trailer: Shot: Beat Aligner - 3D fly-in, punch on beat 3, whip out"). The project's `HISTORY.md` lists the same
steps in order.

## 10. Export

Export ▸ **Max quality** at 1920×1080, 60 fps. The Max length of 1:00 stops the export on the last bar.

## Download {#download}

**[Simple Editor 1.4.0 Trailer project (.zip)](#)** *(link coming with the release)*

Inside:

- `Simple Editor 1.4.0 Trailer.sedit` - the project, with every clip, keyframe, effect, label and comment.
- the screen recordings and the sound effects, inside the folder so it opens on any PC.
- `Montserrat-Black.ttf` - the font of every title (SIL Open Font License). Install it (right-click ▸ Install)
  before you open the project, or the titles fall back to Arial.
- `HISTORY.md` - every step in order.
- `trailer-build.py` - the same edit as a script that drives Simple Editor's MCP server (the same one the [Assistant](./assistant) uses), so you can
  rebuild it with your own takes and song.

If the clips show as offline after you unzip it somewhere else, run **Relink Media…** (Ctrl+P, type Relink) and pick the
project's folder: every file is found by name.

The song is not included. The **Music** track has a comment where it goes: drop your own song there from 0:00, then
open the Beat Aligner and move the shots if your song's tempo is not 100 BPM.

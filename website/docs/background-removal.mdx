---
title: Background removal
description: Cut a person or object out of any video or picture without a green screen.
---

**Remove Background** cuts the subject out of a video or picture clip, no green screen needed. It is an
optional add-on: the models are downloaded the first time you use them, and the app itself stays small.

## Remove a background

1. Right-click a video or picture clip on the timeline ▸ **Remove Background** ▸ **Fast** or **Best**
   (or use the **Remove Background** section of the [Inspector](inspector)).
2. The first time, the app offers to install the add-on. It downloads it, checks every file against its
   checksum and shows the install in **Jobs**.
3. The matte is computed as a job (**Jobs** shows it, with Cancel). The clip plays **unkeyed until its
   frames are ready**, and keyed frames appear as they finish whenever the preview is paused.

Only the part of the file the clip shows (plus half a second each side) is computed. The mattes are kept
in the cache, so trimming, undo, copying the clip or opening the project again does not compute them again.

| Quality | Model | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Fast** | MODNet | People: talking heads, vlogs, webcam footage. Quick. |
| **Best** | BiRefNet | Any subject (objects, animals, products), finer edges. Slower. |

## Controls

The Inspector's **Remove Background** section:

- **Quality**: Fast or Best. Switching computes the other model's frames (both are kept).
- **Matte**: what is ready. *Not installed* (with **Install**), a progress bar while it computes (with
  **Cancel**), *N / M frames* (with **Compute** for the rest), or *Ready*.
- **Edge**: below 0 shrinks the matte for a harder edge (good against a halo), above 0 feathers it.
- **Show Matte**: shows the matte itself (white is kept, black removed), to check it.
- **Off** turns it off (the computed frames stay in the cache).

Background removal runs before the clip's effects, so a **Chroma Key** or a mask still works on top of it,
and the clip blends over the tracks below like any picture with transparency. Exports use the same mattes.

## Speed

Time per frame of a 1080p clip on an RTX 2060 SUPER (GPU through DirectML) and its 8-core CPU:

| Quality | GPU | CPU |
|---|---|---|
| Fast (MODNet, 512 px) | SPEED_FAST_GPU | SPEED_FAST_CPU |
| Best (BiRefNet, 1024 px) | SPEED_BEST_GPU | SPEED_BEST_CPU |

The GPU path works on any DirectX 12 graphics card; without one the CPU takes over by itself. Playback
never runs the models: it only reads frames that are already computed.

## Install, uninstall, licences

**Settings ▸ General ▸ Background removal** installs Fast or Best and uninstalls the add-on. Everything goes
into `%APPDATA%\SimpleEditor\bin\bgremove`; mattes live in the cache folder (`mattes\`), cleared with the
rest of the cache.

| Part | Size | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `se-matte.exe`, the helper | 0.5 MB | MIT |
| ONNX Runtime 1.22 (DirectML build), from Microsoft's NuGet package | 18 MB | MIT |
| DirectML | (part of Windows) | Microsoft, ships with Windows 10 1903 and later |
| MODNet (Fast) | 25 MB | Apache-2.0 |
| BiRefNet lite (Best) | 224 MB | MIT |

Nothing is sent anywhere: the footage never leaves your computer.

![The Library](/img/tutorial/library.png)

The **Library** holds everything in your project: video, audio, images, sequences and folders. It's
on the left of the Edit page (<kbd>Ctrl+1</kbd> shows or hides it).

## Import media

- Click **+ Import** in the Library's header, or press <kbd>Ctrl+I</kbd>, and pick files.
- Or drag files from Explorer onto the Library.
- **Right-click an empty spot ▸ Import from URL…** downloads a video from YouTube, TikTok, X, Vimeo or a
  direct link. This needs [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) (`winget install yt-dlp.yt-dlp`);
  the item only appears once it's found. **File ▸ Import ▸ From URL…** does the same.
- **Image sequences**: import one numbered frame (png, tif, bmp, tga, dpx or exr with at least three
  numbered siblings) and Simple Editor turns the whole sequence into one clip.
- **Timelines from other editors**: **File ▸ Import ▸ Import Timeline (Premiere / Resolve XML, EDL)…**.

Importing doesn't copy or change your files: the project points at them where they are. A dot next to an
item means it's used in the timeline.

## Find things

The header is one row:

- **Search** filters by name, description and tags.
- **Filter ▾** narrows by kind (Video, Audio, Image, Seq, Short SFX, Music), by label, or to
  **Unused only**. The button shows how many filters are on; **Clear filters** turns them off.
- **View ▾** switches between **List** and **Gallery**, changes the thumbnail **Size**, sets the sort
  order, and (in List view) picks the **Columns**. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll also resizes thumbnails.
- **Project | Browse** under the header switches between your project's media and **Browse**, which shows
  your recent files and any folders you've linked (right-click ▸ **Link folder…**). Double-click a file in
  Browse to add it to the project.

With a search or filter on, **Save filter…** keeps it as a **Smart Bin** you can click later.

## Use a clip

- **Click** an item to open it in the **Source** monitor (the tab next to Preview), paused on its first
  frame.
- **Drag** it onto the timeline to place it. Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> while dropping to insert it (later clips
  move right), <kbd>Alt</kbd> to overwrite what's there, or <kbd>Shift</kbd> to put it on a new track on
  top.
- Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (with the mouse over the Library) to add the selected items at the playhead.
- Double-click a **sequence** to open it for editing.

Select several items with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click, or by dragging a box across
empty space. <kbd>Up</kbd> / <kbd>Down</kbd> move the selection and <kbd>Delete</kbd> removes it from the
project (with Undo).

## Right-click menus

![Right-click an item](/img/tutorial/library-item-menu.png)

**Right-click an item** for **Open in Source**, **Add to timeline at playhead**, **Info…** (description,
tags, label and folder), **Label**, **New Subclip from Marks**, **Reveal folder**, **Regenerate proxy**,
**Convert To** (mp4, mov, gif, mp3, wav, png and more), **Convert…**, **Compress…** and **Remove from
project**. If the file is missing you also get **Relink Media…**.

![Right-click an empty spot](/img/tutorial/library-empty-menu.png)

**Right-click an empty spot** for **Import Media…**, **Import from URL…**, **New folder**, **New
sequence**, **Add Adjustment Layer**, **Sort**, **Columns**, **Link folder…**, **Consolidate Media…**,
**Remove unused**, and **Open Project…**.

Folders, sequences and Smart Bins have their own small menus (rename, delete). See the
[right-click reference](right-click.mdx#library) for all of them.

## Organise

- **Folders**: right-click an empty spot ▸ **New folder**, then drag items onto it. Right-click a folder to
  rename or delete it.
- **Labels**: right-click ▸ **Label** gives an item a colour. **Edit labels…** renames them.
- **Info…** holds a description and comma-separated tags, which the search box also looks in.
- **Missing files** show a warning icon. Right-click ▸ **Relink Media…** and pick the folder they moved
  to; files are matched by name, then by length.
- **Consolidate Media…** (right-click an empty spot) copies every file the project uses into the
  project's folder, so you can move or archive it. Save the project first.
- **Remove unused** removes everything the timeline doesn't use.

## The Source monitor

![The Source monitor with In and Out marked](/img/tutorial/source.png)

The **Source** monitor plays one clip on its own, so you can pick the part you want before it goes
into your edit. It has its own playhead and its own In and Out marks.

1. Click a clip in the Library. It opens in the **Source** tab, next to Preview.
2. Play it with <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd> / <kbd>L</kbd>, and press <kbd>I</kbd>
   and <kbd>O</kbd> to mark the part you want. The marked part is shaded on the scrub bar.
3. Put the timeline's playhead where the clip should go, then press **Insert** (<kbd>Shift+V</kbd>, later
   clips move right) or **Overwrite** (<kbd>B</kbd>, replaces what's there). They're the two buttons at
   the right of the Source's transport.

The keys go to whichever monitor you used last: click the Source to drive it, click the Preview or the
timeline to go back. The Source shows an accent outline while it has the keys.

![Right-click the Source monitor](/img/tutorial/source-menu.png)

**Right-click the Source** for more ways to place the marked part:

- **Append at End** puts it after the last clip.
- **Ripple Overwrite** replaces the clip under the playhead and moves later clips by the difference.
- **Close Up** closes the gap under the playhead on one track.
- **Place on Top** puts it on a new track above everything.
- **Source Tape** plays the selected Library items (or all of them) back to back, so you can shop for
  shots.
- **New Subclip from Marks** saves the marked part as a new Library item.
- **Close Source** empties it.

**Match Frame** (<kbd>F</kbd>) does the opposite trip: it opens the clip under the timeline's playhead in
the Source, on the same frame. **Reveal in Library** (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+R</kbd>) selects its file in the
Library.

## Subclips

A subclip is a named part of a longer file, listed under its parent in the Library.

- From the Source: mark In and Out, then right-click ▸ **New Subclip from Marks**.
- From the Library: right-click an item ▸ **New Subclip from Marks** uses the **timeline's** In and Out.

Right-click a subclip ▸ **Rename** to name it.

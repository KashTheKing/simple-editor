The **Classic 2000s** pack is our own take on the look of the home-movie editors of the early 2000s: a
royal-blue title card with white rounded lettering, hearts and keyholes and checkerboards between shots,
sepia and old film, and a button that turns a pile of photos into a slideshow. Everything in it is a normal
transition, effect or text clip, so you can change any part of it afterwards.

It works offline: the first time the app starts, it copies the pack into your library folder.

## Find it

Open the **Gallery** and pick **Classic 2000s** in the category menu of the **Transitions**, **Effects** or
**Titles** tab.

- **Transitions**: Pixel Dissolve, Fade Through Black and Fade Through White, Bars, Blinds, Checkerboard,
  Diagonal Wipe, Clock Wipe, the Circle / Diamond / Heart / Star / Keyhole / Eye irises, Page Turn, Pixelate,
  Rectangle Zoom, Flip, Roll, Shatter In, Sweep, Whirlwind, Ripple, Spiral, Zig-Zag and Bow Tie. Click one
  to put it on the selected cut, or right-click for the start or end of the clip.
- **Fade in or out from black or white**: put **Fade Through Black** or **Fade Through White** on the start or
  end of a clip (right-click the card ▸ start / end).
- **Effects**: Sepia Tone, Black and White, Old Film, Film Age, Soft Blur, Posterize, Watercolour,
  3D Ripple, Mirror and Edge Detect.
- **Speed Up / Slow Down**: Gallery ▸ **Speed** has Speed Up, Double and Quadruple, and Slow Down, Half and
  Quarter.
- **Titles**: Classic Blue Title (the signature card), Basic (a drop shadow over your video), Fade, Fly-In,
  Typewriter, News Banner, Zoom, Spin, Moving Title, Credits Roll and The End. Click one to place it at the
  playhead and type your own words in the Customize panel.

The titles use **Comic Sans MS** when it is installed on your PC. When it isn't, they use **Comic Neue**,
a free look-alike that comes with the app.

## Make a slideshow

1. Select the photos and clips you want, in order: in the **Library**, or on the timeline.
2. In the Gallery, open **Titles** ▸ **Classic 2000s** and click **Make Slideshow**.

Each photo slowly pans and zooms (in on one, out on the next), a different classic transition sits on every
cut, a blue title card opens it and scrolling credits close it. It is one undo step.

To pick the transition, change the card texts, add music or fit the show to a song, ask the
[Assistant](assistant) or use the `slideshow.make` tool:

- `transition`: `random` (the default), `none`, or one name from `slideshow.transitions`, such as `classic-heart`.
- `title` and `credits`: the cards' text (an empty text leaves the card out).
- `music`: a song from your Library, and `fit_to_music: true` to stretch or shorten the photos so the
  slideshow ends with the song.
- `photo_secs` and `transition_secs`: how long each photo and each transition lasts.

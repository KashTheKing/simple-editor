## Media properties

Right-click a Library item ▸ **Properties…** (the Info window has the same button, and the <kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd>
palette has **Media Properties…** for the selected clip). The window lists:

- the **container**: format, duration, size, overall bitrate and the file's tags (title, encoder, creation time…);
- each **stream**: codec and profile, resolution, frame rate, pixel format, **colour** (primaries, transfer,
  matrix, and whether it's limited or full range), rotation, field order, sample rate, channels, language.

**Copy** puts it all on the clipboard as text, handy for a bug report or a client.

### Bitrate graph

**Show Graph** measures the video stream (or **Audio**) second by second: one bar per second, a line at the
average and a tick at every keyframe. Hover a bar for its time and bitrate. Long files take a few
seconds: the file is read once in the background, and the window stays usable.

### Rotation and range

Under **Decode** (Library videos only):

- **Rotation** turns the picture regardless of what the file says: **From file**, or 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°.
  Use it when a phone video comes in on its side. A quarter turn swaps the clip's width and height.
- **Range** says how to read the video's levels. Some phones and screen recorders store full-range video but
  label it limited (blacks look grey and washed out): pick **Full (0-255)**. The reverse (crushed blacks,
  clipped whites): pick **Limited (16-235)**. **As tagged** goes back to the file's own label.

Both apply to every clip of that file, in the preview and the export, as one **Undo** step. A video with an
override always decodes through FFmpeg and skips its proxy.

## Full-range export

**Settings ▸ Export ▸ Levels ▸ Full range** writes SDR H.264 and HEVC exports with 0-255 levels, tagged as full
range. Leave it off for YouTube, social platforms and TVs, which expect the usual 16-235.

## Network streams

**File ▸ Import ▸ Open Network Stream…** opens a stream by its address:

- **HTTP(S)**, including **HLS** (`.m3u8`) and **DASH** (`.mpd`) playlists,
- **RTMP**, **RTSP** (IP cameras), **SRT**, **UDP** and **RTP**.

Press **Open** (or <kbd>Enter</kbd>): the window says whether the stream is live or how long it is, and its
codecs and size, and shows a live preview of the picture. **Record to Library** saves the stream as it comes
in, without re-encoding, so it keeps up with any bitrate; tick **Stop after** to end it on its own. When it
stops, the file (Matroska, `.mkv`) goes into the Library. Recordings are saved in a **Streams** folder inside
your recordings folder (Settings ▸ Capture). The **Recent** list remembers the last ten addresses.

A live stream takes a few seconds to start: if you stop it before the first picture arrives, nothing is saved
and the window says so.

## Search in recent projects

The Home page's **Projects** list has a **Search** box: type any part of a project's name or folder (several
words narrow it down). **File ▸ Open Recent** gets one too once it holds more than six projects.

## Choosing a GPU

On a laptop with built-in and separate graphics, or a PC with two cards, **Settings ▸ Performance ▸ GPU**
picks which one Simple Editor uses: **Automatic** (Windows decides), **Power saving**, **High performance**, or
a card by name. The preview and hardware decoding move together, and the choice applies the next time you
start Simple Editor. It's the same setting as Windows' **Settings ▸ System ▸ Display ▸ Graphics** for this app.

## Contour ShuttlePRO

Plug in a Contour **ShuttlePRO**, **ShuttlePRO v2** or **ShuttleXpress**; there's nothing to install. A
message says when it's connected.

- The outer **shuttle ring** plays forwards or backwards; turn it further for faster (¼× up to 16×). Let go
  and playback pauses.
- The inner **jog wheel** steps one frame per click.
- The **buttons** run actions. Out of the box: Mark In, Mark Out, Clear In / Out, Add Marker, then Go to Start,
  Previous Cut, Play / Pause, Next Cut and Go to End (the ShuttleXpress's five buttons), then Split, Ripple
  Delete, Undo, Redo, Zoom Out and Zoom In. Change any of them in **Settings ▸ Hotkeys ▸ Jog / Shuttle ▸
  Buttons**, or switch the device off there.

![The Beat Aligner with a detected grid, tapped marks and sections](/img/tutorial/beat-aligner.png)

The **Beat Aligner** panel marks out a song so you can cut to it. It finds the tempo and the beat grid, lets you tap
marks while the song plays, and turns a stretch of the song into a named, coloured section (Intro, Verse, Chorus,
Drop ...). Then it uses the marks: timeline markers, cuts, a sound on every mark, clip edges snapped to them, or a
zoom punch on each beat.

It is a tab on the **Audio** and **Cut** pages. On any other page, add it with a tab bar's **+** (Beat Aligner).

## Pick the song

Select an audio clip on the timeline, or pick a song from the list at the top left (every audio clip on the
timeline and every audio file in the Library). **Music** finds beats; **Voice** marks a voiceover instead (below).

A song from the Library that is not on the timeline yet can be marked and detected, but it cannot play there: press
**Add to timeline** to put it on an audio track at the playhead.

## Automatic beats

**Detect** finds the tempo (BPM), the beat grid and the downbeats. It reads the same analysis of the song the
audio visualizers use, worked out once in the background, so it never slows playback. The ruler over the waveform
shows bar numbers on the downbeats and a tick on every beat (eighths and sixteenths when you zoom in).

The grid is yours to correct:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **BPM** | drag or type the tempo |
| **½** / **×2** | half time / double time, when the detection heard the tempo at the wrong speed |
| **Tap tempo** | click it on the beat a few times while the song plays: the tempo is the average of your clicks, and the last click becomes a downbeat |
| **Offset** | where a downbeat is, in seconds into the song |
| **Beats per bar** | 4 for most songs, 3 for a waltz |

Scroll over the waveform to zoom, Shift+scroll to move along it. Click it to move the playhead there.

## Tap along while it plays

Turn on **Tap keys** and press **Play**. Every press of **Space**, **B** or **M** drops a mark where you are in
the song (Space taps instead of pausing while Tap keys is on; use the Pause button to stop). The **Tap** button
does the same with the mouse.

A tap is the player's position when the key arrived, minus your **Latency**: the time between hearing a beat and
the key reaching the editor. Press **Calibrate**, play the song and tap Space on the beat 8 times: the latency is
set to how late your taps landed on the detected grid.

- **Snap** pulls each tap toward the nearest grid line; the slider is how far (all the way = exactly on it), the
  list picks beats, eighths (1/8) or sixteenths (1/16).
- **Continuous** marks every beat of the grid as the song plays past it. **Fill grid** marks them all at once.

## Fix the marks

- Click a mark to select it; Ctrl+click adds to the selection; Ctrl+A selects them all. **Delete** (with the pointer over the waveform) removes
  the selected marks.
- Drag a mark to move it (the whole selection moves). With Snap on it lands on the grid; hold **Alt** to drag freely.
- Drag across empty waveform to select a stretch of the song (and the marks in it).
- Every tap, drag, delete or section is one step of **Undo**. A continuous run is one step for the whole play.

## Sections

Select a stretch (drag across the waveform, or select two marks) and press **Intro**, **Verse**, **Pre-chorus**,
**Chorus**, **Drop**, **Bridge**, **Break** or **Outro**, or type any name and press **Add**. Each name has its own
colour. The list under the buttons shows every section with its time; click one to rename it or change its colour,
✕ deletes it.

## Use the marks

**Use every** picks every mark, every 2nd, every 4th ... for the buttons below.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **To timeline markers** | a timeline marker per mark (coloured by kind: taps yellow, downbeats orange, beats grey, phrases green) and a range marker per section with its name and colour |
| **Cut at marks** | splits the picture at the marks: **All video** cuts every video track, **Selected track** the track of the selected clip. The song itself is never cut |
| **Add at marks** | puts the sound you pick (an audio file from your Library, e.g. a hit or a whoosh) on every mark, a hair early so it lands with the beat, each one stopped before the next, all on one audio track |
| **Apply on marks** | an animation block on each mark: **Zoom Punch**, **Shake**, **Pulse**, **Flash** or any block from the Animator, on the selected clips (or every video clip under a mark), never longer than the gap to the next mark |
| **Snap selected clips to marks** | moves each selected clip's start to the nearest mark and trims its end to the nearest mark (within a quarter second), as long as nothing else on the track is in the way |

![Cut at every beat: the picture is split on the marks, the song is not](/img/tutorial/beat-aligner-cut.png)

### Marker files

**Export CSV** writes the marks and sections as `time,duration,name,note,label` (the Markers panel's own format, so
**Import** in the Markers panel reads it too). **Export EDL** writes a marker EDL that DaVinci Resolve imports as
timeline markers. **Export chapters** writes `00:01:23 Chorus` lines for a YouTube description (the sections, or the
marks when there are none). **Import…** reads any of the three: ranges come back as sections, single times as marks.

## Voiceovers

Choose **Voice** and press **Detect**: instead of a beat grid you get a green **Phrase** mark where speech starts
after a pause and a grey **Pause** mark where it stops. Tap, drag, sections and every button under **Use the marks**
work the same, so you can cut or punch in on each phrase.

## For agents

Everything above is also an MCP tool: `beats.source`, `beats.detect`, `beats.grid`, `beats.tap`, `beats.get`,
`beats.edit`, `beats.section`, `beats.apply` (markers, cut, sfx, snap_clips, effect), `beats.export`,
`beats.import` and `beats.calibrate`. Times in and out are timeline seconds. The `/sync-to-beat` skill uses them.

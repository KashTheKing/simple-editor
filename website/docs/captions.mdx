![The Subtitles panel](/img/tutorial/subtitles.png)

Subtitles live in the **Subtitles** panel (<kbd>Ctrl+6</kbd>; it's next to the Mixer on the Audio page).
Once a project has subtitles, they also show as a **Subtitles** lane at the top of the timeline.

## Add subtitles by hand

1. Move the playhead to where a line should appear.
2. Click **+ Add** (or press <kbd>Alt+S</kbd>). A two-second subtitle appears, ready to type in.
3. Type the text. Change the start and end times in its row, or drag it in the timeline's subtitle lane
   (drag the middle to move it, the edges to change its length).

Click a row to select it and jump to it; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+drag selects more.
**Right-click a row** for **Play cue**, **Split at playhead** (when the playhead is inside it), **Convert
to text clip** (an editable, animatable text clip) and **Delete**; they act on every selected subtitle.

## Transcribe automatically

Simple Editor writes subtitles for you with [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp), which
runs on your own PC.

1. **Get whisper.cpp once**: download a release from its GitHub page and put `whisper-cli.exe` in
   `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SimpleEditor\cache\whisper`, next to `simple-editor.exe`, or anywhere on your `PATH`.
   Click **Re-check** in the panel afterwards.
2. Click **Transcribe…** in the Subtitles panel to open its options. Pick a **Model**: `base.en` is a good
   start, `tiny.en` is fastest, `small.en` most accurate, and `base` understands other languages. The
   first time, **Get captions** downloads it (75–466 MB, once).
3. Select the clip with the speech on the timeline.
4. Click **Transcribe & generate subtitles**. It runs in the background; the subtitles appear when it's
   done, even if you close the panel.

You can also right-click a clip ▸ **Transcript ▸ Transcribe Selected Clip**, or use **Timeline ▸ Auto ▸
Transcribe Selected Clip**.

The options set how lines are broken: **Line length**, **Lines per cue**, **Min duration**, **Pause
split**, **Max words** and so on. Changed your mind? **Regenerate cues** rebuilds the subtitles from the
same transcription without running it again. The transcription also finds **double takes** (a sentence
said twice); mark them on the timeline or cut the extra ones.

## Style

Click **Style ▾** for the font, size, colour, outline, background box, margin, alignment, spacing and
shadow of every subtitle. The **Gallery ▸ Captions** tab has ready-made styles (Bold Outline, Boxed,
Yellow Pop, Karaoke…) that you apply with one click.

## Burn in or export

The **⋯** menu (also on a right-click in the empty part of the list) has:

- **Import…** reads an `.srt` or `.vtt` file.
- **Export SRT…** / **Export VTT…** save the subtitles as a file for YouTube or a video player, and **Open
  folder** writes one next to your project and shows it.
- **Burn in** draws the subtitles into the picture, in the Preview and in every export.
- **To text clips** turns subtitles into text clips on a "Subtitles" track, so you can style and animate
  each one.
- **Delete in range** removes the subtitles between the In and Out marks; **Clear all** removes all of
  them (with Undo).

## Edit by text: the transcript

The Subtitles panel's **Transcript** tab shows the words of a transcribed clip.

- **Click a word** to jump there.
- **Drag across words** (or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click) to select them, then press <kbd>Delete</kbd> (or
  right-click ▸ **Cut selected words**) to cut that part out of the video. Subtitles and markers move along.
- The find box searches every transcribed clip; ◀ ▶ step through the hits.
- The **⋯** menu picks the **Clip**, transcribes the selected clip, and handles **fillers**: **Mark
  fillers** puts a marker on every *um*, *uh*, *like*, *you know*… so you can check them, **Remove fillers**
  then cuts them all, and **Edit filler words…** changes the list and the padding kept around each cut.
- **Speak text…** (also in ⋯) reads text aloud with a Windows voice and places it at the playhead, and
  **Export Transcript…** saves the words as a file.

**Timeline ▸ Auto ▸ Remove Filler Words** does the same as Mark fillers, and Remove fillers the second time.

## The subtitle lane

![Right-click a subtitle in the timeline](/img/tutorial/timeline-cue-menu.png)

In the timeline's **Subtitles** lane, click a subtitle to jump to it, drag it to move it and drag its
edges to change its length. Right-click one for **Split at Playhead**, **Convert to Text Clip**, **Delete
Cue**, **Delete Cues in In/Out Range** and **Clear All Subtitles**; right-click an empty part of the lane
to add one or import a file.

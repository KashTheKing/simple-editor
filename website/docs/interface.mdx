The window has three parts: the **menu bar** at the top, with the **page buttons** in its middle, and
the **panels** below it. Each panel sits in a group with tabs along its top.

## Pages

Pages are ready-made arrangements of panels for one kind of work, in the same order as Resolve's pages.
Switch with the buttons in the middle of the menu bar, **Window ▸ Pages**, or <kbd>Alt+1</kbd>–<kbd>Alt+6</kbd>.
The **Inspector** is in the top-right corner of every page.

| Page | Key | What's on it |
|---|---|---|
| **Media** | <kbd>Alt+1</kbd> | Library · Source · Inspector, full height. Bringing in, sorting and watching media. |
| **Cut** | <kbd>Alt+2</kbd> | Library · Source / Preview · Inspector on top, the Timeline below. Quick cutting. |
| **Edit** | <kbd>Alt+3</kbd> | Library (with Effects, Transitions, Gallery) · Source / Preview · Inspector, the Timeline below. The page the app opens on. |
| **Color** | <kbd>Alt+4</kbd> | Gallery (Looks, LUTs) · Preview · Inspector, a thin Timeline, then Nodes / Curves · Scopes. |
| **Audio** | <kbd>Alt+5</kbd> | A small Preview · Mixer / Subtitles · Inspector, with a tall Timeline for audio tracks. |
| **Export** | <kbd>Alt+6</kbd> | Export settings · Preview · Inspector over Jobs (the render queue), with the Timeline below. |

Every page remembers its own arrangement. Changing the Color page never touches the Edit page.

**Make an arrangement the page's default** with **Window ▸ Layout ▸ Save as This Page's Default**. Then:

- **Reset Page Layout** (in **Window ▸ Layout**, or right-click the page's button) goes back to your
  default, or to the built-in one if you haven't saved one. <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> undoes it.
- **Reset to Built-in Layout** goes back to the page as it came with the app.

## Panels

| Panel | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Library** | Your imported media, sequences and folders ([Library](library.mdx)) |
| **Source** | Watch a clip from the Library and mark the part you want ([Source monitor](library.mdx#the-source-monitor)) |
| **Preview** | The program monitor: what your edit looks like ([Viewer](viewer.mdx)) |
| **Timeline** | Where you arrange and cut clips ([Timeline](timeline.mdx)) |
| **Inspector** | Settings of whatever is selected ([Inspector](inspector.mdx)) |
| **Effects**, **Transitions**, **Gallery** | Things to add to clips ([Effects, transitions & titles](effects.mdx)) |
| **Mixer**, **Subtitles** | Audio levels and buses ([Audio](audio.mdx)); captions ([Captions](captions.mdx)) |
| **Scopes**, **Nodes**, **Curves** | Color tools ([Color](color.mdx)); Curves is also the keyframe graph |
| **Export**, **Jobs** | Export settings and the render queue ([Export](export.mdx)) |
| **Markers**, **Auto-cut**, **Tracking**, **Planner**, **Moodboard**, **History** | Extra tools, hidden until you open them ([Advanced](advanced.mdx)) |

**To open a panel that isn't showing**, use any of these:

- Click the **+** at the right end of a tab bar. It lists every panel that isn't on screen and adds the
  one you pick to that group.
- Tick it in the **Window** menu. The less common ones are under **Window ▸ More**.
- Press its shortcut, like <kbd>Ctrl+1</kbd> for the Library (see [shortcuts](shortcuts.md#panels--pages)).
- Type its name into the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd>).

![The + button lists hidden panels](/img/tutorial/add-panel-menu.png)

### Right-click a tab

![Right-click a tab](/img/tutorial/tab-menu.png)

- **Maximise panel** fills the window with that panel. Do it again (it now says **Restore panel**) to
  go back. Double-clicking a tab does the same, and so does <kbd>`</kbd> (the key left of 1) with the
  mouse over a panel.
- **Undock** moves the panel into its own window, for example onto a second monitor. Keyboard
  shortcuts keep working there. Close that window to dock the panel back where it was.
- **Close** hides the panel. The Window menu brings it back.
- **Stay on this tab** stops the group from switching away from this tab when you select something (see
  below). A pinned tab shows a pin.
- **Set Icon** changes the tab's icon.

### Moving and resizing panels

Drag the gaps between panels to resize them.

**Drag a tab** to move its panel: nine drop squares appear over the panel under the pointer. Drop on the
middle one to add it as a tab there, or on an outer one to split the space in that direction.
<kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> undoes a move. Once you like your layout, **Window ▸ Layout ▸ Lock Panels** stops a stray
drag from moving anything (tabs still switch, and the gaps still resize).

Undocked windows open where you last left them, at the same size.

### Saving layouts

**Window ▸ Layout** also has **Save Profile…**, **Load Profile**, **Export Profile to File…** and
**Import Profile from File…** (a `.sedit-layout` file), to keep arrangements you like, switch between
them on any page, or share them.

![The Window menu with Layout open](/img/tutorial/window-menu.png)

### Panels that follow your selection

When you select something, the group that edits it comes to the front: a video or text clip shows the
**Inspector**, an audio clip the **Mixer**, a transition **Transitions**, a subtitle **Subtitles**. If
you'd rather have panels stay put, turn off **Settings ▸ General ▸ Switch panel tabs to follow the
selection**, or pin a single tab with **Stay on this tab**. With it off, the tab that would have
switched glows briefly instead.

## The menu bar

| Menu | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **File** | New, open, recent projects, save, **Import ▸**, **Export ▸**, **Record ▸** (voiceover, screen), Settings |
| **Edit** | Undo and redo, cut / copy / paste (with **Paste Special ▸**), delete, ripple delete, select, **Find…** |
| **Clip** | Split, duplicate, enable, link, speed, freeze frame, **Add ▸** (text, shape, adjustment layer, mask, subtitle), **Transition ▸**, nest, multicam, match frame |
| **Timeline** | **Mark ▸** In and Out, lift and extract, **Trim ▸**, markers, **Auto ▸** (auto-cut, beats, fillers, ducking, loudness, auto colour), **Tracks ▸**, zoom, snapping |
| **Playback** | Play, J / K / L, loops, stepping, jumping to cuts, proxies, playback resolution, fullscreen |
| **Window** | Pages, every panel, social guides, **Layout ▸** (lock, save and reset layouts), maximise |
| **Help** | This tutorial (**Tutorial & Docs**), the keyboard shortcut list, the command palette, what's new |

Every menu item shows its shortcut, and right-clicking one lets you give it a different icon.

## Find any command: Ctrl+K

![The command palette](/img/tutorial/palette.png)

Press <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> and start typing. The **command palette** searches every command, every panel
("Show Mixer"), and your scripts. <kbd>Enter</kbd> runs the highlighted row; greyed-out rows say why
they can't run right now. With nothing typed, your recent commands come first.

Some commands live only here, for example **Restore Autosave…**, **Fast Review** and **Trim View in
Preview**.

## See every shortcut: F1

![The keyboard shortcuts window](/img/tutorial/cheatsheet.png)

<kbd>F1</kbd> opens **Keyboard Shortcuts**: every command by group with its key, the keys that can't
be changed, and all the timeline's mouse gestures. Type in its search box to filter. The same list is
on the [keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md) page.

## Settings

![Settings, Hotkeys tab](/img/tutorial/settings-hotkeys.png)

**File ▸ Settings…** (<kbd>Ctrl+Comma</kbd>) has six tabs:

- **General**: theme, where FFmpeg and yt-dlp are, autosave, snapping, the "follow the selection" and
  home-card switches, the Explorer right-click entry, fonts, and the MCP server for AI co-editing.
- **Performance**: GPU preview, preview quality, the playback cache, proxies, movie mode.
- **Capture**: screen recording and voiceover inputs.
- **Export**: default encoder, quality and speed preset.
- **Hotkeys**: rebind any command. Click **Rebind**, press the keys (<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels,
  <kbd>Backspace</kbd> unbinds). A **Keymap preset** switches to Premiere, Resolve or Avid style keys,
  and the keyboard map shows what's taken.
- **Appearance**: Cozy (rounded) or Sharp look, 25+ built-in themes, your own colours, clip colours, a
  background image, and icons. Themes can be exported and imported as `.sedit-theme` files.

The **Device Emulator** shows your video the way your viewers will see it: playing inside a real phone,
tablet, browser window or TV, with the platform's own app round it. The icons are the platforms' real
ones, taken from their own pages, and the layouts follow the apps.

Open it from **right-click the Preview ▸ Emulator**, from **Window ▸ Device Emulator**, or from a tab bar's
**+**. It is a normal pane: dock it beside the Preview, put it on a second screen, or make it a tab.

## The bar at the top

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Device** | 101 devices, grouped by maker and kind, with a search box; each row shows its size in points (dp on Android) and its real pixels. Every iPhone: Dynamic Island (14 Pro to 17 Pro Max and iPhone Air), notch (X to 14 Plus and 16e) and home button (the original iPhone to 8 Plus and SE 1st to 3rd gen, drawn with the home button and the 20 pt status bar). Pixel 6 to 10 and Galaxy S23 to S25 and A35 to A55 (Android status bar, punch-hole camera, gesture or three-button bar), iPads with and without a home button, desktop and laptop sizes from 1366 x 768 to 4K and the MacBooks, a TV. |
| **Platform** | YouTube (long form), YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, an Instagram post, Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight, an X post. |
| **Landscape / Portrait** | Turns a phone or tablet. YouTube becomes its full-screen player; the vertical apps show their full-screen player with the action buttons down the right edge. |
| **Comments** | Opens the platform's comments, drawn the way that app draws them. |
| **Captions** | Shows or hides your project's subtitles, the same switch as the player's **CC** button. |
| **Gallery** | Every device and platform at once, each playing your video. Click one, or pick a device, platform or orientation, to bring it up large. |
| **Screenshot** | Saves a picture of the device to `Pictures\Simple Editor`. |
| **Export** | Exports the timeline, or the In/Out range, as a video inside the device: the device frame and the platform UI round the playing video (its progress bar and time follow the video), with the project audio. It runs as a background export (Jobs pane, Cancel), the file is the device frame's size, and it opens in your media player when it is done. |

The pane remembers the device, platform and orientation **for each project**.

## Click it like the real thing

In the large view, the device works:

- **Play / pause**: the play button, or a tap on the video. It plays the editor itself.
- **Scrub**: click anywhere on the progress bar to jump there.
- **CC**: YouTube's captions button (and the settings menu's **Subtitles/CC** row) turns your project's
  subtitles on or off. It is one undo step.
- **Settings**: YouTube's gear opens its real settings menu (Ambient mode, Annotations, Audio track,
  Subtitles/CC, Sleep timer, Playback speed, Quality); on a phone it is the app's settings sheet.
- **Speaker**: mute, and click along the volume slider to set it (the preview's own speaker; your edit's
  audio is not changed).
- **Theatre / Fullscreen** on YouTube desktop: the player glides to the theatre layout or fills the
  window. The Default view button (in theatre) goes back.
- **Comments, Share, the description, the profile, the sound**: tap them to open their sheets. On YouTube
  desktop the page scrolls down to the comments instead, as on youtube.com.

### What each platform shows

- **YouTube desktop**: the top bar (logo, search, voice search, Create, notifications, your avatar), the
  player with its control bar (play, next, the speaker and its slider, the time, captions, settings,
  miniplayer, theatre, fullscreen), the title, the channel row with **Subscribe**, the like / dislike pill,
  **Share** and the more button, the description, and the comments (count, **Sort by**, **Add a
  comment...**, each comment with like, dislike and **Reply**). The left menu stays closed.
- **YouTube on a phone**: the app's player (previous / play / next, captions, settings, the time,
  fullscreen, the red bar) and the page under it; comments open as the app's sheet.
- **Shorts, TikTok, Reels**: the action column with the real icons and your profile's counts, the
  creator row, the caption, the sound row (TikTok's spinning record), the comment sheets in each app's own
  style (TikTok's is white, with a heart and count on every comment).

### Buffering

When the editor's player has to wait for frames, the device shows that platform's own spinner: YouTube's
white arc, Instagram's spokes, X's blue ring, TikTok's pulsing line along the bottom.

### Your profile

The names, picture, counts and comments come from your [social profiles](social-guides#profiles). Every
avatar is clipped to its circle.

## For scripts and the assistant

- `emulator.open {device, platform, orientation, gallery, sheet, view}` shows the pane on a device and
  platform (anything left out stays as it is).
- `emulator.export {device, platform, orientation}` starts that export as a job and returns the file.
- `emulator.screenshot {device, platform, orientation, sheet, view, menu, buffering, width}` returns a
  picture of it with the frame at the playhead.

The [social guides](social-guides) (safe zones and the UI overlay over the Preview, and burning the guide
into an export) are still there; the Emulator is the place to look at the whole app.

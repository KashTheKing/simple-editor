title: Templates
description: Animated templates you fill with your own footage, the Template Builder for making your own, and sharing a template as one file.

A **template** is a ready-made piece of timeline: clips on their tracks with their animation blocks,
keyframes, transitions, titles, shapes, HTML layers, effects and sound. Its **slots** are the clips you fill
with your own footage. Everything a template places is ordinary clips and blocks, so you can change any of
it afterwards in the Animator and the Inspector.

Templates live in **Gallery ▸ Templates**: the built-in ones first, then yours (marked **M**).

## Use a template

1. Optional: select the photos or videos you want in the **Media Library** (Ctrl / Shift-click for several).
2. Click a card in **Gallery ▸ Templates**. A template without slots is placed at the playhead straight away.
3. A template with slots opens **Use Template**: one row per slot with what it wants (image, video, any),
   its length and the footage chosen for it. The media you had selected is already filled in, in order.
   - **Use selected media** fills the slots again from the Media Library's selection.
   - Drag media from the Media Library onto a row (several at once fill that slot and the ones after it).
   - **…** on a row picks a file from disk; **✕** empties the slot.
4. **Place** puts the finished sequence at the playhead as **one undo step**.

### Drag it onto the timeline

The quickest way: drag a template card onto the timeline. It lands where you drop it as **one sequence clip**
(one undo step, no window). Double-click it to go inside: every slot is a container, and the beat edits
(Velocity Edit, Hype Montage, Meme Cut, Wasted) come with their own sample footage already in them. Their
song and sound effects are fixed parts of the template: nothing asks you for them. Only the video shots are
slots.

**Wasted** turns your shot into the game's WASTED moment: grey, a slight slow-mo and a slow zoom under a
dark band with the word. The word uses the **Pricedown** font when it is installed (it is free to use in
your videos, but its licence does not let the app include it), otherwise Impact. For the sting, put your
own sound at `%APPDATA%\SimpleEditor\gallery\templates\media\wasted\wasted.m4a`, with the sting 0.6 s in. That footage is trimmed to what the template uses, is fetched
from the online library the first time you drop it, and goes into a Library bin of its own,
**Templates/&lt;name&gt;**. Dropping the template again reuses that bin.

To swap a slot's footage, drop a Library clip on it (on the timeline, or on the **Media** row in the
Inspector). The slot keeps its in point, speed, effects and timing. A shot the template uses more than once
is **linked**: replacing it once replaces it everywhere. The Inspector shows how many slots share it;
**Unlink** gives one slot its own footage.

A slot you leave empty stays on the timeline as a placeholder with a coloured card under it. Fill it later
with right-click ▸ **Replace Container Media…**.

### How footage fits a slot

| The slot says | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Trim footage** (the default) | The slot keeps its length. Longer footage is cut to it, shorter footage is slowed down to fill it. A photo simply shows for the slot's length. |
| **Stretch template** | The slot takes the length of your footage. Everything after it moves along, a caption over it grows with it, and an animation at the slot's end stays at its end. |

A slot plays the sound of your footage when the clip it was made from had sound. The built-in templates'
slots are picture only.

## Built-in templates

| Template | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Photo Slideshow** | Four photos that drift slowly: the first pops in, the next ones push in from alternating sides or dissolve, with a title |
| **Beat Montage** | Six short shots: a punch on every cut, a shake on every second one |
| **Title Intro** | A title, a line and a subtitle over slowly moving footage |
| **Product Showcase** | A hero shot that drops in, two details pushed in from the sides, a name and a price tag |
| **Before and After** | Two shots side by side with a divider and a label each |
| **Quote Card** | A quote over dimmed footage, the author under a short line |
| **Lower Third Opener** | Footage with a name bar that slides in and leaves again |
| **Vertical Short Opener** | A hook line that pops and shakes, two fast clips and a caption |
| **End Card** | A thank-you, two video cards for what to watch next and a button |
| **Photo Grid** | Four photos that pop into a grid one after another |

They are built for the size of your project (wide, square or tall) from the Animator's own blocks and the
app's shapes and text, so every part can be restyled.

## Template Builder

**Clip ▸ Save as Template…** (or right-click an empty spot in the Gallery ▸ **Template Builder…**) opens the
builder on the selected clips, or on the whole timeline when nothing is selected.

- **Name** and **Tags**, as for any Gallery item. A name that exists is replaced.
- **From**: the selection or the timeline.
- **Picture at**: the frame shown on the template's Gallery card. Moving it moves the playhead so you see it.
- **Footage**: one row per footage clip.
  - **▣ Slot** makes the clip a slot. Only video clips can be slots: pictures, sound and music stay part of
    the template. Open the row to give the slot a name, say what it wants and choose
    **Trim footage** or **Stretch template**. The order of the rows is the order slots are filled in:
    drag a row's handle, or use **▲ ▼** (Alt+Up / Alt+Down).
  - **🎞 Footage** chooses whether the clip's footage is **Included** in the template or replaced by a
    **Placeholder**. A placeholder is always a slot. For a sound clip the choice is Included or Left out.
- Text, shapes and other layers stay as they are.

**Save** puts it in **Gallery ▸ Templates** as one of yours.

## Share a template

Right-click one of your template cards:

- **Export with footage…** writes one `.setemplate` file holding the template and the footage it includes,
  each file trimmed to the part the template uses.
- **Export without footage…** leaves the video footage out: every video clip becomes an empty slot. Its
  sound, music and pictures still ride along, trimmed to what the template uses.

The file never contains a folder path of your PC, only file names.

To add a template someone sent you, use **Import…** in the Gallery's **⋯** menu or drop the `.setemplate`
file onto the window. Its footage is unpacked into your gallery folder
(`%APPDATA%\SimpleEditor\gallery\templates\footage\`), footage that is not in the file becomes empty slots,
and a name you already have is numbered.

## Scripts and assistants

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `templates.list` | Your templates and the built-in ones |
| `templates.slots` | A template's slots: name, what it wants, length, fit |
| `templates.use` | Place a template with a slot → media mapping (asset ids or file paths), one undo step |
| `templates.build` | Save clips of the timeline as a template, with slots and placeholders |
| `templates.export` / `templates.import` | Write / read a `.setemplate` file |

**Window ▸ Find a Reference** (or right-click the Library ▸ **Find a Reference**) opens a panel for the clips you
quote, react with or point at in a video.

## Search

Type what you're looking for: a line someone said, a reaction ("facepalm"), or a scene. The buttons below open that
search on **Yarn**, **PlayPhrase**, **Clip.cafe**, **Tenor**, **GIPHY** or **YouTube** in your browser. The best fit
comes first: quote sites for a line, GIF sites for a reaction, YouTube for anything else. Hover a button to see what
the site is good for.

## GIPHY and Tenor in the app

Pick **GIPHY** or **Tenor** and press **Search** (or Enter) to see results right in the panel. Click one to import it
into the Library as an MP4. This needs your own free key: get one from the service (**Settings ▸ General ▸ Get a
Key**) and paste it into **GIPHY key** or **Tenor key**. Only your search words and your key are sent, and only when
you search.

## Import a link, or just a part

Paste a link and press **Import** (this needs yt-dlp, see **Settings ▸ General ▸ yt-dlp folder**). Tick **Just a
part** and type a range like `1:23-1:45` to download only those seconds. A link that starts at a time (`?t=83`) fills
the range in for you. The same **Just a part** option is on **Import URL**.

## Your references

Everything imported from this panel:

- keeps where it came from (the page, the site, the title and the uploader),
- gets a `reference` tag and appears in the Library's **References** smart bin,
- is transcribed in the background (when whisper and a model are installed), so typing a quote in the Library's
  search or in **Search by quote** here finds it. Turn this off with **Settings ▸ General ▸ Transcribe references**.

**Copy Credits** copies one line per reference (title, site, uploader, the part used and the link), ready for a
description or an end card.

## Fair use

The first import shows a short note: clips from films, shows and GIF sites belong to their owners, and short excerpts
for commentary, criticism, review, parody or teaching can be fair use depending on how and where you publish. Keep
clips short and credit them. Simple Editor only ever downloads the one link you chose: it never scrapes or
bulk-downloads.

## For the Assistant

`references.suggest` finds matching references already in the project and the best search links,
`references.search_links` gives (or opens) the search addresses, and `references.import_range` imports a link or a
part of it (it asks you first, since it downloads). `references.list`, `references.set` and `references.credits` read
and edit the records.

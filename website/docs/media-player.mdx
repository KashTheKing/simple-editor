---
title: Media player
description: The fast player a video or song opens in from Explorer - reverse, loop, speed, audio tracks, metadata and song covers.

![The media player](/img/tutorial/player.png)

Open a video or a song from Explorer (right-click ▸ **Edit with Simple Editor**, or double-click it when
Simple Editor is the app that opens it) and it plays in Simple Editor's own media player, not the editor. The first frame is on screen in about half a
second. The player uses your theme's colours and accent from **Settings ▸ Appearance**.

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or click the pencil at the top left, to take the file into the editor at the same moment.

## The controls

The bar at the bottom hides with the mouse cursor after a moment and comes back when you move the mouse.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Play / Pause (the big round button) | <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd>; a click on the picture does the same, a double-click goes full screen |
| Back / Forward 10 | skip 10 seconds (<kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd>) |
| Previous / Next | the other files in the same folder (<kbd>Ctrl+←</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+→</kbd>) |
| Shuffle | play the folder in a random order |
| Loop | play the file again from the start (also while reversing) |
| Reverse | play backwards in real time, at the chosen speed, with the sound backwards too |
| **1×** | playback speed, from 0.25× to 4× |
| CC | subtitles and audio tracks |
| Speaker | volume (<kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd>, <kbd>M</kbd> mutes) |
| Mini player, Full screen | a small window that stays on top; full screen is also <kbd>F</kbd> or <kbd>F11</kbd> |

The seek bar has a tick for every second and smaller ones between. Rest the mouse on it to see a thumbnail
of that moment; the thumbnails load in the background, the end of the file first.

**Rename** the file by clicking its name at the bottom left, typing and pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd>. If the
file is in an open project's Library, the Library follows it.

## Audio tracks and Audio Only

A file with more than one audio track (a game recording with the game and your microphone, say) lists them
all under the CC button. All of them play by default; tick the ones you want to hear, any combination, or turn
them all off. The choice is remembered per file.

**Audio Only** hides the picture and shows a visualizer of the sound instead. Pick its style and how strongly
it reacts in **Visualizer style**.

## Songs

![A song with its cover](/img/tutorial/player-song.png)

A song opens in Audio Only, with its cover art over a blurred copy of it (or the visualizer, if you picked
that last time). Covers and thumbnails show a soft shimmer while they load.

## The right-click menu

![The player's right-click menu](/img/tutorial/player-menu.png)

Right-click the picture for everything on the bar plus:

- **Resolution**: the playback size, **Auto** (the picture's size on screen) or a fixed number of lines.
  Remembered per file.
- **Screenshot frame** saves the frame you see as a picture. The message that follows has **Open**,
  **Open Folder** and **Add to Project**.
- **Convert…** saves the file in another format.
- **Upscale…** makes a sharper, bigger copy with the AI enhancer (see [Advanced](advanced.mdx)).
- **Properties / Metadata…** (below).
- **Rename**, **Open in Editor** and **Show in folder**.

## Metadata

![Properties / Metadata](/img/tutorial/player-metadata.png)

**Properties / Metadata…** edits the file's tags: title, author, genre, copyright, description, comment,
and for songs artist, album, track and the rest. Change the thumbnail or a song's cover from a picture or
**Use the current frame**. **Tags** are the Windows tags you see and search for in Explorer (M4A, MP4, MOV,
WMA and WMV files can hold them). **Save** writes them into the file without re-encoding it; **Clear all
metadata…** removes every tag.

**Metadata presets** save tags you use again and again (your name as author, a genre, a copyright line):
**Save as preset…** here, or make them in **Settings ▸ Metadata**. Apply one with **Apply preset** here,
from a Library item's right-click menu, or in **Export ▸ Apply preset** so every export gets it.

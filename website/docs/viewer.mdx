![The Preview with a clip selected](/img/tutorial/viewer.png)

The **Preview** shows your edit at the playhead. It's also where you move and resize things: drag on
the picture instead of typing numbers.

## Play and move around

The row under the picture has, from left to right: the **timecode**, the transport buttons (**Go to
Start**, **Step Back 1 Frame**, **Play / Pause**, **Step Forward 1 Frame**, **Go to End**), the
**zoom** menu (**Fit**, 50 %, 100 %, 200 %) and **fullscreen**. Above it, a thin scrub bar grows when you
hover it; click or drag it to jump.

**Click the timecode to type a time**: `1:30`, `00:01:30:12`, `+10` (frames) or `-2s` all work.
<kbd>Enter</kbd> jumps there, <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

| To… | Press |
|---|---|
| Play / pause | <kbd>Space</kbd> |
| Play backwards / forwards, faster each press (1×, 2×, 4×, 8×) | <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>L</kbd> |
| Stop | <kbd>K</kbd> |
| Step one frame | <kbd>Left</kbd> / <kbd>Right</kbd> (<kbd>Shift</kbd>: 10 frames) |
| Jump to the previous / next cut | <kbd>Up</kbd> / <kbd>Down</kbd> |
| Jump to the start / end | <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> |
| Mark In / Out | <kbd>I</kbd> / <kbd>O</kbd> |
| Clear In and Out | <kbd>Alt+X</kbd> |
| Go to In / Out | <kbd>Shift+I</kbd> / <kbd>Shift+O</kbd> |
| Play In to Out, or loop it | <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Space</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+L</kbd> |
| Play a couple of seconds around the playhead | <kbd>/</kbd> |
| Fullscreen | <kbd>F11</kbd> or double-click the picture; <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves |

In and Out marks show on the timeline's ruler. They're used by Lift, Extract, Loop, and by export if you
tick **Export In/Out Range**.

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll zooms the picture, dragging with the middle mouse button pans it, and **Fit**
resets.

## The tool rail

![The Shape tool's menu and options bar](/img/tutorial/viewer-shapes.png)

The column of buttons at the top-left of the picture picks what a drag on the picture does:

| Tool | Key | Drag on the picture to… |
|---|---|---|
| **Select** | <kbd>V</kbd> | Move the selected clip, and use its handles (below) |
| **Crop** | | Drag the selected clip's edges to crop it |
| **Text** | <kbd>T</kbd> | Add a text clip there (5 seconds long, at the playhead) |
| **Shape** | <kbd>Shift+S</kbd> cycles | Draw a rectangle, ellipse, triangle, polygon, star, line or arrow. Click the button to pick one |
| **Draw** | <kbd>D</kbd> | Draw strokes by hand; the record button draws while the video plays |
| **Mask** | <kbd>G</kbd> | Draw a mask on the selected clip: rectangle, ellipse, polygon or path |

While a shape or draw tool is on, an **options bar** along the top sets the fill, stroke, stroke
width, corner radius, number of sides or brush. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while drawing a shape to keep
its proportions (lines snap to 45°), and <kbd>Alt</kbd> to draw it out from the centre. For a polygon,
click each corner and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (or click the first point) to close it.

## Move, scale, rotate and crop

Select a clip on the timeline and it gets a box with handles in the Preview (with the **Select** tool):

- **Drag inside** the picture to move it.
- **Drag a corner** to scale it evenly; **drag an edge's middle** to stretch it one way.
- **Drag the knob above the top edge** to rotate it. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to snap to 15° steps.
- Turn on **Crop Handles** (right-click ▸ Crop Handles, or the **Crop** tool) and the edge handles crop
  instead.

While you drag, the clip **snaps** to the centre, edges and thirds of the frame and to other clips,
with a guide line to show it. Turn that off with right-click ▸ **Snap to Canvas**. For exact numbers,
use the [Inspector](inspector.mdx). If the clip is animated, a drag sets a keyframe at the playhead.

## Right-click the picture

![Right-click the picture](/img/tutorial/viewer-menu.png)

Right-clicking a clip selects it. Then, for that clip: **Reset Transform**, **Fill Frame**, **Stretch to
Frame**, **Crop Handles**, **Add Mask to Selection** and **Add Text Here**. Always there:

- **Stop**, **Mark In**, **Mark Out**, **Clear In / Out**.
- **Zoom** and **Playback Resolution** (100 % down to 25 %: lower is smoother on a slow PC).
- **Proxies**: play light-weight copies of big video files. On by default; they're built in the
  background and never used for export.
- **Movie Mode (pre-render)**: render ahead so heavy effects play smoothly.
- **Social Guides**: safe-area outlines for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and more.
- **Show / Hide Scopes**, **Background** (checkerboard, black, white or a colour) and **Snap to Canvas**.

## Multicam angles

When the selected clip (or the clip under the playhead) is a multicam clip, an **Angles** list appears
in the Preview's top-right corner. Click an angle to cut to it at the playhead. Make a multicam clip
from two or more clips of the same moment with **Clip ▸ Create Multicam from Selection**; they're
synced by their sound. Close the list with its ×, and bring it back with **Clip ▸ Multicam Angles…**.

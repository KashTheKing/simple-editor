![The Export page](/img/tutorial/page-export.png)

**File ▸ Export ▸ Export Video As…** (<kbd>Ctrl+E</kbd>) takes you to the **Export** page:
the export settings on the left, the Preview in the middle, and the **Jobs** list (the render queue) with the
**Performance** tab on the right. Export needs FFmpeg.

## Export a video

![The Export panel](/img/tutorial/export-panel.png)

The panel reads top to bottom: pick a **preset**, check the project settings and the **Video**, **Audio** and
**Advanced** sections if you want to, then press **Export…** in the bar at the bottom. The project settings
(name, format, resolution, frame rate, colour, [Max length](project-settings.mdx#max-length), background) are
at the top of the panel, so you can change them without leaving the page.

1. Click a **preset card**. The selected card is outlined in your accent colour (and says *edited* once you
   change something it set).
   - **Project settings — Max quality**: exactly your project's size and frame rate, at the best quality.
   - **Fast preview**: the same size and frame rate, encoded as fast as your computer can (the graphics
     card's encoder when it has one), with a smaller file - for checking an edit.
   - The platform cards: **YouTube 1080p**, **YouTube 4K**, **Shorts / Reels / TikTok** (1080×1920) and
     **Instagram 1:1**. The picture is fitted inside that size with black bars, never stretched.
   - **My presets**: your own (see below).
2. Optional: open a section to change something (each remembers whether you left it open).
3. Check the summary line at the bottom (size · frame rate · length · estimated file size, measured from short
   samples of your timeline), pick a **Speed** (Fast, Balanced or Small file), the file name and the folder.
   **Target size** (for example 50 MB) aims the file at a size instead of a quality, for sites with an upload
   limit.
4. Click **Export…**, confirm the name and the format, and save.

The export runs in the background with a progress bar and time left in the **Rendering** panel (it comes up
when an export starts; **Cancel** is there) and the **Jobs** list, so you can keep
working. **Add to Queue** picks a file name now and renders it after whatever is already running; the
**Jobs** list shows the order, and ▲ / ▼ change it. **Cancel** stops an export at once and deletes the
unfinished file; cancelling the running one also empties the queue. When it is done, the message has **Open**,
**Open Folder** and **Add to Project**.

With a [Max length](project-settings.mdx#max-length) set and a timeline longer than it, the export stops at the
limit; a line in the panel says how much is left out, with a button to include it.

### Your own presets

Set things up the way you like, then click **+ Save current settings…** under *My presets*: give it a name and,
if you like, a platform icon or a colour. It stores the size, format, quality, encoder, speed and audio
layout. Right-click any card for **Duplicate**; right-click one of yours for **Rename / icon / colour…**,
**Update with current settings** or **Delete**. The two built-in cards and the platform cards can't be
changed - duplicate one to make your own version. **Quick Export** (<kbd>Ctrl+M</kbd>) re-runs whichever card
you exported with last, yours included.

Exports are small by default: the graphics-card encoder uses lookahead, B-frames and adaptive quality, and its
quality scale matches x264's, so a file is about a third of what 1.3 made.

### Video

**Resolution** (project size, 720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K or custom), the **Frame rate** (Project, 24, 25, 30, 50,
60 or Custom; the timeline is resampled to it),
the **Format** (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, GIF, or MP3 / WAV / M4A / FLAC for sound only) and the **Quality**,
with a warning when it is low. **Fast lossless cut** appears when the timeline is a plain cut of one video:
instant, no quality loss, but cuts snap to the video's keyframes.

An HDR project (**Color** HDR10 or HLG) exports 10-bit HEVC, or AV1 for WebM; GIF, AVI and 8-bit encoders are
refused with a message (see [Project settings](project-settings.mdx#color-sdr-or-hdr)).

### Audio

**Mix**, **Separate tracks in file** or **WAV stems** (see [Export audio tracks](export-audio-tracks)), and
**Normalize loudness (−14 LUFS)**, on by default: evens out the loudness for YouTube and friends.

### Advanced

Closed at first.

- **Export In/Out range only**: only the part between your In and Out marks.
- **Write metadata**: title and other tags in the file. **Apply preset** fills them from a metadata preset
  (Settings ▸ Metadata).
- **Use project background**.
- **Burn in captions**: draws the subtitles into the picture. Until you change it here it follows the preview's
  subtitle toggle (what exports always did); untick it to deliver captions as a separate .srt instead.
- **Burn in social guide**: draws the Social Guide chosen in the Preview over every frame, with its progress bar
  and time following the export (see [Social guides](social-guides)). Greyed out until a guide is chosen.
- The **Scaler**, the **Encoder** (including NVIDIA, Intel and AMD hardware encoders if your graphics driver has
  them), the encoder **Preset** and Smart render.

Hover over any option for a short explanation.

## Quick Export

The **Quick Export** button at the top right of the window (also **File ▸ Export ▸ Quick Export…** and
<kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd> `>Quick export`) asks for a file name and type (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, GIF, WAV or MP3), then
exports in the background with the Export panel's current settings, or the project's own size, frame rate and
quality, and opens the file in your media player when it is done. It remembers the folder and type per project.

For a video you opened directly (one clip, maybe with text or captions on top) the dialog starts on that file.
Saving over it asks **Replace &lt;name&gt;?** first, and the original is kept as `<name>.bak` until the new file
is in place.

## Quick ways out

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Quick Export** (<kbd>Ctrl+M</kbd>) | Exports the whole timeline again with the settings of your last export, next to the project file (or the video you opened), without asking |
| **Export Frame…** (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+F</kbd>) | Saves the frame at the playhead as PNG, JPG or WebP, at the project size or larger |
| **Fast Lossless Cut…** (<kbd>Ctrl+Alt+E</kbd>) | Cuts a single video without re-encoding it |
| **Export Premiere / Resolve XML…** (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+E</kbd>) | A timeline file you can open in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve (see [Hand off](handoff.mdx)) |
| **Export Avid EDL…** | CMX 3600 EDL files for Avid Media Composer (see [Hand off](handoff.mdx)) |
| **Export Markers…** | Your markers as a CSV file (the Markers panel can also export YouTube chapters and Avid markers) |
| **Overwrite Original Video…** | For a project opened from one video: replaces that file with your edit (asks first) |

All of these are in **File ▸ Export**.

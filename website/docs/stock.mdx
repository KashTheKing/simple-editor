---
title: Stock footage
description: Search free stock video, pictures, music and sound effects from the Gallery and add them to your project with their credit.
---

**Gallery ▸ Stock** searches free stock media online and adds what you pick to your project. It works on
both sides of the Gallery's Video | Audio switch: pick **Video**, **Images**, **Music** or **SFX** above the
results.

1. Type what you want and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> (or **Search**).
2. Narrow it down: **shape** (landscape, portrait, square), **minimum size** (720p, 1080p, 4K) and
   **licence**. The licence filter starts at **CC0 / PD**: nothing that needs a credit.
3. Rest the pointer on a card to preview it: a video plays a few frames, a sound plays a short, quiet
   snippet (turn previews off in the **⋯** menu or in **Settings ▸ Stock**).
4. Click a card for its details: title, author, licence, source page and the credit text.
5. **Add** downloads it as a background job (see the **Jobs** panel) into the project's **Stock Footage**
   folder and adds it to the Library. **Add at playhead** also puts it on the timeline. You can also drag a
   card straight onto the timeline: it is placed at once and fills in when the download finishes.

Every stock file keeps its credit: the asset's description in the Library holds the attribution, licence
and source page, the asset is tagged `stock`, and a `<file>.stock.json` file sits beside the download.
**⋯ ▸ Export credits…** writes a text file with the credit of every stock file in the project, the ones
you must credit first. Paste it into your video's description.

Searches are remembered for ten minutes and thumbnails load only for the cards on screen. With no
internet connection the tab says so.

## Sources

No account and no key are needed for the first four. Each site is searched through its official,
documented API; nothing is scraped. Simple Editor tells the sites only its own name and version (its
User-Agent), never anything about you.

| Source | What | Key | Licences you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) | video, pictures, sounds | none | CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA (per file) |
| [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/prelinger) | films (the Prelinger Archives and other public-domain or CC films), music | none | Prelinger films are public domain; other items by their CC licence |
| [NASA Image and Video Library](https://images.nasa.gov) | video, pictures, audio | none | NASA media is not copyrighted in the US. Don't use the NASA logo or imply NASA endorses you ([NASA's guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)) |
| [Openverse](https://openverse.org) | pictures, music, sound effects | none (anonymous, rate-limited) | CC0, Public Domain Mark, CC BY, CC BY-SA |
| [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/api/) | video, pictures | your own free key | Pexels License: free to use, no credit needed, don't sell unaltered copies |
| [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/api/docs/) | video, pictures | your own free key | Pixabay Content License: free to use, no credit needed, don't sell unaltered copies |
| [Freesound](https://freesound.org/apiv2/apply) | sound effects, music | your own free key | CC0, CC BY (the high-quality MP3 preview is downloaded) |

Licences that forbid commercial use (NC) or changes (ND) are never shown: an edit is a change, and a video
may be monetised. Items with no clear licence are left out too.

### Your own keys

Pexels, Pixabay and Freesound need a free API key of your own. Get one with **Get a key** in
**Settings ▸ Stock**, paste it there and click **Save**. The key is stored in Windows Credential Manager on
this PC (not in the settings file) and is only ever sent to that site's API. Simple Editor ships no key of
its own.

### More sites

Mixkit, Coverr, Videvo, Videezy, the Free Music Archive, Incompetech, the YouTube Audio Library and BBC
Sound Effects have no public API (or terms that forbid automated access), so they are only links: **⋯ ▸
More sites** and **Settings ▸ Stock** open them in your browser. Read each site's licence before you use
what you download there.

## Settings ▸ Stock

- **Sources**: switch each one on or off; the keyed ones turn on when their key is saved.
- **Save in the project's folder**: downloads go to `Stock Footage` beside the saved project. Unsaved
  projects use the app's own `Stock Footage` folder (`%APPDATA%\SimpleEditor\Stock Footage`).
- **Preview on hover**.

## For agents (MCP)

- `stock.search` `{query, kind: video|image|music|sfx, orientation, min_res, licence: pd|by|by-sa, page}`:
  runs in the background and replies with the results (ids, titles, licences, attributions).
- `stock.add` `{id, place_at?}`: downloads a result, imports it and replies with the new asset ids, the file
  and its attribution.
- `stock.credits`: the project's credits text.

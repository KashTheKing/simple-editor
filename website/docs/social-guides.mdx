Every platform draws its own buttons, captions and bars over your video. The [Platform Emulator](device-emulator)'s
guides show you
where, measured on the real apps' own pages, so a Short, TikTok or Reel keeps its important parts clear
of them.

Right-click the Preview ▸ **Platform Emulator** and pick a platform; untick **Show Guides on Preview** to hide
it. The platform and device are the Platform Emulator pane's too: pick one in either place and both follow. The guide only ever draws over the picture: it never changes your project, and your export
only when you tick **Burn in platform guide** in the Export settings (see below).

## Three ways to look

| Mode | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Safe zone** | The parts the app covers in red, parts it sometimes covers in amber, the safe area outlined in green, and a percentage on each. A dashed outline is what a profile-grid thumbnail keeps. |
| **UI preview** | The app's buttons drawn over your video as plain silhouettes (action column, avatar, caption, sound row, progress bar), filled in with your profile. |
| **Device preview** | Your video inside a drawn phone, tablet or desktop browser, with the app's UI on top, and **everything clickable** (see below). |

Under the platform list:

- **Device**: iPhone (19.5:9, Dynamic Island), Android (20:9, punch-hole), a small 16:9 phone, a tablet
  and a desktop browser. A phone's status bar and home indicator are shown as system bars in Safe zone mode.
- **Preview as**: which of your [profiles](#profiles) the guide shows.
- **Worst case of all platforms**: one safe area that works on every platform of the same shape at once
  (all the vertical ones, for a Short you post everywhere).
- **Maintain Aspect Ratio**: draw each guide at its platform's own shape (9:16 on a 16:9 project, dimming
  what gets cropped), or stretch it over the whole picture.
- **Draw in render.frame and contact sheets**: off by default. When on, `render.frame` and
  `render.contact_sheet` carry the guide, which is handy for sharing a preview.

## Device preview

Click things like you would on the phone:

- **Comments**: a sheet slides up from the bottom. YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels **shrink
  the video into the part above the sheet**; the others dim it and slide the sheet over it. Click the **X**,
  the grabber or the dimmed video to close it.
- **The caption or "… more"**: the description sheet, with your caption, hashtags and counts.
- **The avatar** or **channel name**: a profile sheet. **The sound row** or disc: a sound sheet.
  **Share** and **⋮ more**: the share and menu sheets.
- Long form (YouTube) and feed posts (Instagram post, X) get the page around the player: title, channel
  row, description and comments, which open under the player.
- The player follows the editor: the progress bar and the time ("0:42 / 3:51") track the **playhead** as you
  play, scrub or seek, and the play button shows pause while the editor plays. The total length is the whole
  timeline (what a full export gives you); it counts in hours once the timeline is an hour long. This works in
  every mode (UI preview and Device preview).

### Burning a guide into an export

Export ▸ Advanced ▸ **Burn in platform guide** draws the chosen guide over every exported frame, as if the video
were playing in the app: the pause button shows, and the progress bar and time follow the exported video (the
In/Out range when you export one, else the whole timeline), frame by frame. A Device preview burns in as its UI
preview, so the export keeps your project's size. The editor's own labels (the guide's name, "Measured …") and the
crop dimming are left out. Off by default; Overwrite Original never burns a guide in.

Each sheet shows the sample comments from your profile.

### Desktop player: default, theatre, fullscreen

Pick **Desktop browser** as the device and YouTube (long form) as the platform, and the player has the three
views of the real site, all measured on it:

- **Default**: the video on the left (70 % of the page), the related videos on the right, title, channel row,
  description and comments under it.
- **Theatre mode**: a player as wide as the page; a 16:9 video is 84 % of the page with black bars at the sides, and the
  related videos move below.
- **Fullscreen**: the browser and the page disappear and the video fills the screen with the title bar, the control
  bar and the progress bar.

Click the **theatre** or **fullscreen** button in the drawn control bar, press <kbd>T</kbd> or <kbd>F</kbd> while the
pointer is over the preview (<kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves fullscreen), or use right-click ▸ Platform Emulator ▸ Guide Options ▸ **Player view**.
The player glides between the views. A project that is not 16:9 is letterboxed or pillarboxed inside the player, as on
the site. (Mini-player is not drawn.)

Everything stays inside the device: the screen is clipped to its rounded corners and nothing is drawn outside the body.

## Profiles

**Settings ▸ Social** holds fake accounts, one tab per network: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook,
Snapchat and X. A profile has a handle, display name, verified badge, follower count, a sample caption,
hashtags, a sound title, like / comment / share / save / view counts (type anything: these are mock-ups),
a channel link, a picture and a few sample comments. Add as many as you like per network and pick the
one the guide uses with **Use this profile** (●), or from the viewer's **Preview as**.

**No account is ever linked.** Nothing asks for a password, a login or a token, and nothing is stored but
the text you see.

### Fetch from my public profile

Type your public profile address (or just `@handle`) and press **Fetch**. Simple Editor downloads that one
public page, without logging in and without cookies, reads its name, picture and follower text, and shows
you **exactly what it found and where from**. Nothing is applied until you press **Apply to this profile**.

- It only happens when you press the button. Never at start-up, never while you edit.
- Many sites (Instagram, X, Facebook) show no public name or picture without a login. Then you get a plain
  "nothing found" and you fill the fields in by hand.
- **Choose picture…** takes any picture file; it's cropped to a circle and kept inside `settings.json`, so
  the profile moves with your settings. **Export profiles…** / **Import profiles…** do the same through a file.

## How accurate is it?

Each platform's numbers were measured on its real logged-out web pages (browser DevTools: the on-video
buttons, text sizes and sheets) and from the platforms' own safe-zone documents; every platform lists what
it was measured on and the date. Settings ▸ Social ▸ **Guide data** shows the file's date.

What could **not** be measured without logging in, and is an estimate, is marked `est` in the data: the
exact native-app layout of TikTok and Instagram Reels, how Instagram and Facebook's comment sheets move
the video, and the phone-app controls of YouTube long form. If a guide looks out of date, check the app
against it and tell us, or update it yourself.

### Updating the guides without a new release

The guides are one small file, `guides.json`. Put a newer copy in
`%APPDATA%\SimpleEditor\library\guides\guides.json` (Settings ▸ Social ▸ **Open folder**) and platforms
with the same id replace the built-in ones; new ids are added. **Check the library for updated guides**
downloads the library's copy on a click. The format is in `library/README.md`.

## For scripts and agents

`guides.list`, `guides.set`, `guides.preview`, `profile.get`, `profile.set`, `profile.fetch`: set the
platform, mode, device and open sheet, and get a picture of the result (`state: comments` for the comments
sheet open). `profile.fetch` reads a web page, so the Assistant asks you first.

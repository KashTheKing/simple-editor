---
title: Export separate audio tracks
description: Export dialogue, music and effects as their own audio tracks in the video file, or as WAV stems beside it.
---

By default an export has one stereo mix. For a mixer, a translator or a client who wants to change the
music later, the **Audio** row on the Export panel can also give you each part on its own.

## Pick a layout

| Audio | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Mix** (default) | One stereo track, as before. |
| **Separate tracks in file** | The mix first (the default track players use), then one more audio track per stem, each named after it. MP4, MOV, MKV and M4V only. |
| **WAV stems** | The file gets the mix, and each stem is written as a 24-bit, 48 kHz WAV in a folder beside it. |

**Separate tracks in file** is greyed out when the format you picked can't hold several audio tracks. If
you save to such a format anyway, the export writes one mix and says so.

## Split by

When the layout isn't **Mix**, **Split by** decides what one stem is:

- **Track**: one stem per audio track. A muted track (or one silenced by another track's solo) is silent
  in the mix, so it gets no stem.
- **Bus**: one stem per [Mixer](audio.mdx#the-mixer) bus a clip is routed to (the clip's own bus, else its
  track's, else Main).
- **Role**: one stem per clip audio role: **Dialogue**, **Music**, **SFX**, **Ambience**, and **Other** for
  clips without a role.

Each stem sounds exactly as it does inside the mix: after its volume, pan, track and bus settings. Put the
stems together and you get the mix back, except for effects that react to the whole mix (a compressor or
limiter on Main or on a bus several stems share) and crossfades between clips of different stems on one
track.

## Where the stems go

Exporting `My video.mp4` with **WAV stems** creates a folder `My video stems` next to it, with files
numbered in stem order: `01 Dialogue.wav`, `02 Music.wav` and so on. The folder only appears once the whole
export has succeeded.

Scripts and agents choose the same thing with the `audio` (`mix`, `streams` or `stems`) and `stem_by`
(`track`, `bus` or `role`) arguments of `export.video` and `export.queue`.

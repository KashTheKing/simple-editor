![The Gallery's Effects tab](/img/tutorial/effects.png)

The **Gallery** sits next to the Library on the Edit page. Its **Effects** and **Transitions** tabs (and
every other tab) are catalogues of cards: click one to apply it to the selected clips, or **drag it onto the
timeline** and drop it where it should go. What a clip already has is edited in the
[Inspector](inspector.mdx).

## Dragging cards onto the timeline

Every card except a skill can be dragged (press and move a few points; <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels), and where you
drop it decides what happens. The timeline lights up what it will land on while you drag:

| Card | Drop it on |
|---|---|
| Effects, Looks, LUTs, Caption styles, Speed ramps, Animate blocks | a **clip**. With several clips selected and the one you drop on among them, all of them get it |
| Transitions | a **cut** or a clip's **edge**: it snaps to the nearest edge within a few points, else the half of the clip you are over picks start or end |
| Templates, Titles, HTML layers, icons | an **empty part of a video track** (or the empty lane below the last track, for a new track) |

A library item that is not installed yet (cloud mark) downloads first, then does what a drop would have done.
Your own saved items drag the same way: see [My Gallery](my-gallery.mdx).
Dropping anywhere else does nothing.

## Effects

1. Select one or more clips on the timeline.
2. Open the Gallery's **Effects** tab (<kbd>Ctrl+3</kbd>) and **click a card**. It's added to every
   selected clip.

You can also **drag a card onto a clip** on the timeline, or right-click a card ▸ **Add to selected
clip(s)** / **Add to all clips on this track**. Type in the search box to find one: it searches the built-in
effects, yours and the [online library](online-library.mdx) (once you have clicked **Browse online library**)
together, in one grid; the chips and the category menu narrow it (a cloud mark means it downloads when you
click it).

The catalogue has blur, motion blur, pixelate, VHS, glow, vignette, sharpen, chroma key (green screen),
colour replace, levels, curves, hue and saturation, black & white, flip, 3D plane, camera shake,
LUTs and a **Custom Shader** you write yourself in GLSL. Crop is not an effect any more: every clip has
its own (the Inspector's Transform section, or the Preview's Crop tool). Projects with the old Crop
effect still open; it turns into the clip's own crop when that looks the same, and otherwise stays.

### The effect stack

![A clip's effects in the Inspector, with an effect's right-click menu](/img/tutorial/inspector-effects.png)

A clip's effects are listed in the Inspector's **Effects** section, top to bottom in the order they're
applied. Each row is a grip, a checkbox and the effect's name:

- The checkbox turns an effect off without removing it.
- Drag the grip to change the order.
- Click the name to show or hide its settings. Every setting has a **◆** to
  [animate](inspector.mdx#keyframes) it.
- **Right-click the name** for **Add mask** (limit the effect to a shape you draw on the Preview), **Remove
  mask**, **Edit shader…** (Custom Shader), **Copy parameters** / **Paste parameters** (to another clip with
  the same effect), **Move up** / **Move down**, **Open in Node editor** and **Remove**.

**Adjustment layers** (**Clip ▸ Add ▸ Add Adjustment Layer**, <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+L</kbd>) are empty clips whose
effects apply to everything below them on the timeline, for as long as the layer lasts.

### Effect zones

An **effect zone** is an effect stack laid over a stretch of time instead of on one clip: grade or blur
everything from 0:30 to 0:45, whatever clips are there. A zone covers either the **whole timeline** (every track
below it, like an adjustment layer that never gets in the way of your clips) or **one track** (just that track's
clips).

- **Add one**: right-click the ruler ▸ **Add Effect Zone** (the whole timeline), right-click a video track's header ▸
  **Add Effect Zone** (that track), or **Add Effect Zone** in the command palette. It covers the In/Out range,
  else the selected clips, else five seconds from the playhead.
- It shows as a bar in the **Zones** lane above the tracks: teal over the whole timeline, violet on one track
  (its name is on the bar). Overlapping zones stack.
- **Drag the bar** to move it, **drag an end** to make it longer or shorter (both snap like clips), and
  **double-click** it to jump to its start.
- **Click it** and the Inspector shows its card: name, **Applies to** (whole timeline or a track), **From**,
  **Length**, **On**, and its effects, with **Add effect** and the same stack as a clip's (reorder, masks,
  keyframes with **◆**; keyframe times count from the zone's start).
- **Right-click the bar** for **Applies to**, **On** and **Delete Zone**.

Zones play, export and pre-render through the same effect path as clips, so they cost no more than the same
effects on clips would.

## Transitions

![The Gallery's Transitions tab](/img/tutorial/transitions.png)

A transition blends one clip into the next at a cut. The built-in ones are **Cross Fade**, **Fade to
Color**, **Push** and **Wipe**; the online library adds more (they share the grid, with a cloud mark until
installed). The row above the cards sets the **Duration** of the next one you add (1 second to start with;
it is never longer than half of the shorter clip it touches), plus its colour (Fade to Color) or the
direction it comes **From** (Push, Wipe).

- **Drag a card onto a cut** on the timeline. It snaps to the nearest cut or clip edge; away from any edge,
  drop on the right half of a clip for its end, the left half for its start.
- Or **click a card** to put it at the start of the selected clips (a cut where a clip comes before, else a
  fade in from black).
- Or select clips and **right-click a card**: **Apply to Start** and **Apply to End** put a one-sided fade
  on that edge of each selected clip; **Apply to Connection** (shown when the clip touches the next or
  previous one; **(start)** and **(end)** when both do) makes the two-sided transition on that cut; with two or
  more clips selected, **Apply to All** puts it at the beginning of each of them (the cut where a clip abuts
  the start, otherwise a fade in), as one undo step. It replaces a transition already there unless
  `transition_skip_existing` is on in your settings file.
- Or click a cut and press <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+T</kbd> (**Add Transition at Selected Cut**) for a one-second
  Cross Fade. <kbd>Ctrl+T</kbd> adds the last one you used again. Both are in **Clip ▸ Transition** too.

Hovering a card previews it in the Preview, when the selected clip is under the playhead.

**To change a transition**, click its band on the timeline: the [Inspector](inspector.mdx) shows its kind,
duration and easing. Drag the band's edges to make it longer, or right-click it ▸ **Change Type**,
**Change Easing**, **Move To** (the clip's start or end, or the cut with the previous or next clip; the
transition keeps its settings) or **Remove Transition**.

## The Gallery

![The Gallery panel](/img/tutorial/gallery.png)

The **Gallery** holds ready-made presets, one tab each. Click a card to apply it to the selected clips, or
right-click it (**Apply to selection**, **Apply to every caption**, **Place at playhead**) to see it in the
Preview first:

| Tab | What a click does |
|---|---|
| **Animate** | Applies a keyframe block (Fade In, Slide, Pop…) at the playhead |
| **Looks** | A colour grade. It **replaces** the clip's effects. To keep your own, select a graded clip and right-click an empty spot ▸ **Save look from selection…** |
| **Luts** | Adds a `.cube` LUT from your LUT folders |
| **Captions** | Sets the style of all subtitles (Classic, Bold Outline, Boxed, Karaoke…) |
| **Speed** | Gives the clip a speed ramp (Montage, Hero, Bullet, Jump, Flash) |
| **Effects**, **Transitions** | Described above |
| **Templates** | Places a built-in animation template or one you saved (**Clip ▸ Save as Template…**), after you fill its slots with footage: see [Templates](./templates) |
| **Titles** | Places a title (below) |

## Titles and text

![A title clip in the Preview and the Inspector](/img/tutorial/inspector-text.png)

- **A quick text**: **Clip ▸ Add ▸ Add Text Clip** (<kbd>Shift+T</kbd>) puts a 5-second text clip at the
  playhead. Or pick the **Text** tool (<kbd>T</kbd>) and drag on the Preview, or right-click the Preview
  ▸ **Add Text Here**.
- **A designed title**: **Gallery ▸ Titles** has **Lower Third**, **Title Card** and **Caption Box**.
  Click one to place it; a **Customize** box under the cards lets you change its text and colours.
- Type the words, pick the font and style in the [Inspector](inspector.mdx), and drag the text on the
  Preview to place it.
- To animate it, use the Inspector's **Animation** presets (Slide, Zoom, Pop, Fade, Spin), the **Reveal**
  and **Wave** sliders, or your own [keyframes](inspector.mdx#keyframes).
- Save a style you like with the Inspector's **Text style presets** and apply it to other text clips.

### TikTok and Snapchat text

**Gallery ▸ Titles** has a **Social** category (pick it in the category menu, or search "social") with the
looks of the two apps' own text tools. Each one is an ordinary text clip, so the words, colours and every
other setting stay editable in the [Inspector](inspector.mdx).

| Title | Look |
|---|---|
| **TikTok Classic** | bold white text with a thin dark outline and shadow |
| **TikTok Background** | a rounded box behind each line that hugs that line's width; the boxes merge into one shape. White box with black text, plus **Dark**, **Red**, **Yellow** and **Blue** |
| **TikTok Semi-Transparent** | the same boxes in see-through black |
| **TikTok Typewriter** | the classic look, typed in letter by letter over the first 1.2 seconds |
| **TikTok Neon** | white text with a pink glow |
| **Snapchat Bar** | a see-through black band the full width of the frame with centred white text. Drag it up or down on the Preview; it stays full width at any aspect ratio |
| **Snapchat Big Text** | large bold white text with a soft shadow |
| **Snapchat Highlight** | text on a yellow pill |

The fonts come with the editor: **TikTok Sans** (TikTok's own font, released under the SIL Open Font
License) and **Inter** for the Snapchat looks, so they look the same on any PC.

In the Inspector's text settings, **Box** sets the box colour (its alpha is the opacity) and padding,
**Box shape** picks **Block** (one box round the text), **Lines** (a box per line) or **Bar** (the full width),
and **Corner radius** rounds the corners (a big radius makes a pill).

The titles sit in the middle of the frame, inside the safe zone of every platform in the
[Platform Emulator](social-guides.mdx). For captions, **Gallery ▸ Captions** has **TikTok Background** and
**Snapchat Bar** for [subtitles](captions.mdx), so automatic captions can use the same looks.

title: Effects, transitions & titles

![The Effects panel](/img/tutorial/effects.png)

The **Effects**, **Transitions** and **Gallery** panels sit next to the Library on the Edit page. Each is
a catalogue of cards: pick one and apply it to the selected clips. What a clip already has is edited in
the [Inspector](inspector.mdx).

## Effects

1. Select one or more clips on the timeline.
2. Open **Effects** (<kbd>Ctrl+3</kbd>) and **click a card**. It's added to every selected clip.

You can also **drag a card onto a clip** on the timeline, or right-click a card ▸ **Add to selected
clip(s)** / **Add to all clips on this track**. Type in the search box to find one.

The catalogue has blur, motion blur, pixelate, VHS, glow, vignette, sharpen, chroma key (green screen),
colour replace, crop, levels, curves, hue and saturation, black & white, flip, 3D plane, camera shake,
LUTs and a **Custom Shader** you write yourself in GLSL.

### The effect stack

![A clip's effects in the Inspector, with an effect's right-click menu](/img/tutorial/inspector-effects.png)

A clip's effects are listed in the Inspector's **Effects** section, top to bottom in the order they're
applied. Each row is a grip, a checkbox and the effect's name:

- The checkbox turns an effect off without removing it.
- Drag the grip to change the order.
- Click the name to show or hide its settings. Every setting has a **◆** to
  [animate](inspector.mdx#keyframes) it.
- **Right-click the name** for **Add mask** (limit the effect to a shape you draw on the Preview), **Remove
  mask**, **Edit shader…** (Custom Shader), **Copy parameters** / **Paste parameters** (to another clip with
  the same effect), **Move up** / **Move down**, **Open in Node editor** and **Remove**.

**Adjustment layers** (**Clip ▸ Add ▸ Add Adjustment Layer**, <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+L</kbd>) are empty clips whose
effects apply to everything below them on the timeline, for as long as the layer lasts.

## Transitions

![The Transitions panel](/img/tutorial/transitions.png)

A transition blends one clip into the next at a cut. There are four: **Cross Fade**, **Fade to Color**,
**Push** and **Wipe**. The row above the cards sets the **Duration** of the next one you add (1 second to
start with), plus its colour (Fade to Color) or the direction it comes **From** (Push, Wipe).

- **Drag a card onto a cut** on the timeline. Drop on the right half of a clip for its end, the left half
  for its start.
- Or select clips and **right-click a card** ▸ **Add at start of selected clip(s)**, **Add at end of selected
  clip(s)** or **Add at every cut on this track**. At the start or end of the timeline, you get a fade from
  or to black.
- Or click a cut and press <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+T</kbd> (**Add Transition at Selected Cut**) for a one-second
  Cross Fade. <kbd>Ctrl+T</kbd> adds the last one you used again. Both are in **Clip ▸ Transition** too.

Hovering a card previews it in the Preview, when the selected clip is under the playhead.

**To change a transition**, click its band on the timeline: the [Inspector](inspector.mdx) shows its kind,
duration and easing. Drag the band's edges to make it longer, or right-click it ▸ **Change Type**,
**Change Easing**, **Move To** (the clip's start or end, or the cut with the previous or next clip; the
transition keeps its settings) or **Remove Transition**.

## The Gallery

![The Gallery panel](/img/tutorial/gallery.png)

The **Gallery** holds ready-made presets, one tab each. Click a card to apply it to the selected clips, or
right-click it (**Apply to selection**, **Apply to every caption**, **Place at playhead**) to see it in the
Preview first:

| Tab | What a click does |
|---|---|
| **Looks** | A colour grade. It **replaces** the clip's effects. To keep your own, select a graded clip and right-click an empty spot ▸ **Save look from selection…** |
| **Luts** | Adds a `.cube` LUT from your LUT folders |
| **Captions** | Sets the style of all subtitles (Classic, Bold Outline, Boxed, Karaoke…) |
| **Speed** | Gives the clip a speed ramp (Montage, Hero, Bullet, Jump, Flash) |
| **Templates** | Places a clip template you saved at the playhead (**Clip ▸ Save Selection as Template…**) |
| **Titles** | Places a title (below) |

## Titles and text

![A title clip in the Preview and the Inspector](/img/tutorial/inspector-text.png)

- **A quick text**: **Clip ▸ Add ▸ Add Text Clip** (<kbd>Shift+T</kbd>) puts a 5-second text clip at the
  playhead. Or pick the **Text** tool (<kbd>T</kbd>) and drag on the Preview, or right-click the Preview
  ▸ **Add Text Here**.
- **A designed title**: **Gallery ▸ Titles** has **Lower Third**, **Title Card** and **Caption Box**.
  Click one to place it; a **Customize** box under the cards lets you change its text and colours.
- Type the words, pick the font and style in the [Inspector](inspector.mdx), and drag the text on the
  Preview to place it.
- To animate it, use the Inspector's **Animation** presets (Slide, Zoom, Pop, Fade, Spin), the **Reveal**
  and **Wave** sliders, or your own [keyframes](inspector.mdx#keyframes).
- Save a style you like with the Inspector's **Text style presets** and apply it to other text clips.

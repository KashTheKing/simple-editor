The **Animate** page (<kbd>Alt+4</kbd>, after Edit) is for moving things: the Gallery, the Preview, the
Inspector, a thin Timeline and the **Animator** panel. The Animator is also on any page from the Window menu,
<kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd>, or a clip's right-click ▸ **Show Animations**.

## Blocks

An animation in Simple Editor is a **block**: a move from where the clip starts (**From**) to where it ends
(**To**), with an ease for how it travels between them. Every Gallery animation (Fade In, Slide, Pop, Zoom
Punch, Ken Burns…) drops as a block, and every block has controls in the Inspector: **Side**, **Strength**,
**Length** and **Ease**. Change a control and the block rewrites its own keys.

In the Animator each selected clip gets a lane per kind of property its blocks animate (Motion, Opacity,
Speed, Sound). In a lane:

- Drag a block to move it, drag its ends to stretch it. With **Snapping** on it snaps to other blocks and to
  every clip's edges, with a guide line. Blocks that overlap stack in rows.
- Open a block (**▸**) to see its move as one curve: pull the two middle points to shape the ease, an end point
  sideways to stretch it.
- **Split** a block at the playhead (<kbd>Ctrl+B</kbd> with the block picked, or right-click ▸ **Split at
  playhead**).
- Shift / Ctrl-click several blocks, then **Flow together** (each starts where the one before ends, speeds
  matched) or **Link into one block**.
- **Loop** (Inspector or right-click) repeats a block, optionally ping-pong; the bar shows a tick per cycle.

Each kind of animation has its own colour (In teal, Out orange, Physics purple, Collide pink, Emphasis
yellow, Camera blue, Text sky, Sound green, Speed red, Custom slate), on the block, its Inspector pill, its
Gallery card and the strip along the bottom of the timeline clip. The timeline itself no longer draws keys.

## The Blocks tab

The Animate page's Gallery opens on its **Blocks** tab, sorted like the **+ Animation** menu: Transitions, In,
Out, Physics, Emphasis, Camera, Text, Sound, Speed, then your own (Mine). Each card shows the block's move as a
small curve with its kind's colour dot. Clicking into the Animator brings a visible Gallery back to Blocks,
unless you picked another tab since.

- **Click** a card: the block goes on the selected clips at the playhead, like **+ Animation**.
- **Drag** a card onto a clip's lane in the Animator (any view: Blocks, Curves, Flow or Speed) or onto a clip
  on the timeline: it starts where you drop it, on that clip. A ghost shows its length while you drag.
- **Drag a transition** onto the Animator's **Timeline** lane to make a timeline span there, or onto a cut on
  the timeline.

Each drop is one undo step.

**Hand keys**: keys you set yourself with ◆ show as a dashed **Hand keys** row. Click it (or right-click the
clip ▸ **Turn hand keys into blocks**) to turn them into a Custom block with controls; the motion stays the
same. **Record** off means changing a value never adds a key.

## Views: Blocks, Curves, Flow, Speed

All four views share one ruler, playhead, zoom bar (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+wheel to zoom, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+wheel to
pan) and the **Selected clips / Whole timeline** switch on the right.

- **Blocks**: the lanes above.
- **Curves**: the clip's channels with their keys as curves with bezier handles; box-select, ease presets,
  value or velocity. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> while dragging keys to lock to time or value. Select keys ▸
  **Make a block** or **Loop keys**.
- **Flow**: wherever one move hands over to the next, on one clip or across a cut, a ⚡ or ✓ badge shows
  whether the speeds match. **Smooth** eases the next move, the earlier one or both so they meet.
- **Speed**: the clip's speed ramp. Ramps are real time remapping, so they never jump; Speed Ramp In / Out,
  Slow-mo Hit and Speed Up blocks, and "Ramp through the cut".

Drag a row's bottom edge (or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+wheel) for taller graphs.

In **Curves** each clip's **Properties** list folds: the **▼** before its title folds the clip to one line, and
each group (Transform, Opacity, Sound, Speed) folds with its own arrow. The 👁 on a row shows or hides that
property's curve on the graph. Right-click the title, a group or a row to choose which properties are listed:

- tick or untick each property (the menu stays open while you pick),
- **Show All**, **Hide Unkeyed**, **Only Keyed**,
- **Collapse All** / **Expand All**,
- **Reset to Default View**.

Hiding a property only changes the view: its keys stay and still play. The choice is the same on every clip
and is remembered with your settings, along with the folded groups.

## Special blocks

- **Simulated**: Camera Shake (jolts per second, fade out, tilt, hide edges), Spring In, Bounce Drop, Throw
  Out, Wobble.
- **Physics**: Gravity Fall and Throw & Bounce are rigid bodies with Mass, Gravity, Elasticity, Friction, Air
  drag, Velocity and Spin. **Drop with collisions** (two or more pictures selected, **⋯ ▸ Drop with
  collisions**) lets the clips fall, land and push each other apart.
- **Sound**: Duck, Pan Sweep, Pan Swing and Tremolo.
- **Path**: the pencil in the Animator's toolbar (<kbd>D</kbd>). Draw in the Viewer and each selected clip
  travels your stroke; **Timing** is Shape or As drawn, **Smoothing** rounds the stroke, **Turn along path**
  faces the clip the way it goes.
- **Link to layer**: one clip's property follows another's (a multiplier plus an offset).

**Onion skin**: while the Animator is open the Preview shows the selected layer's outline a few frames before
and after.

## Transitions over the whole timeline

The lane at the top of the Animator holds **timeline spans**: a bar there puts its transition on every cut
under it, closes the gaps between the footage clips first, and keeps doing so wherever you move or stretch it.
Delete it and its transitions go. Add one from **+ Animation ▸ Transitions ▸ Timeline** or a right-click in the
lane; its Inspector card sets the transition, duration, ease and direction.

## Stacks and presets

**Clip ▸ Nest ▸ Stack Clip with Overlays** groups a clip and the overlays above it into one nested clip on the
clip's own track, so a transition moves them together.

Right-click a block ▸ **Save as preset** puts it in the Gallery. Presets can be imported, and the preset folder
opened to share the files.

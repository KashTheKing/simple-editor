An **editing style** is a short Markdown file: the measurable rules at the top (average shot length, how many
cuts land on the beat, transitions, caption look, loudness, grade, how the pacing changes) and plain
guidance below. Set one for a project and the [Assistant](assistant.mdx) follows it in every request: it is
written into the project's [Project Context](project-context.mdx).

![The Editing Styles tab](/img/tutorial/editing-styles.png)

## Browse the library

In the Assistant, **⋯ ▸ Editing Styles**. Each row shows a style and whether it is built in, yours, or
applied to this project; the buttons on the right apply, duplicate and delete it. Click a row to preview its
rules and guidance, with **Apply** (or **Remove**), **Duplicate**, **Rename**, **Export…** and **Delete**.
**Import…** adds a `.md` file; **Open folder** shows where yours live (`%APPDATA%\SimpleEditor\styles`).

The built-in styles are **vlog**, **trailer**, **documentary**, **music-video**, **podcast-clips** and
**short-form**. They match the [style skills](editing-skills.mdx) (`/style-vlog` and the rest), which do the
whole editing pass. A built-in can't be changed: duplicate it to make your own.

## /style

Type `/style` and a name in the Assistant's box (it completes the name as you type) and press Enter:

- `/style trailer` writes the trailer style into this project's Style guide and Rules, as one undo step,
- `/style off` takes it out again,
- `/style` alone opens the library.

Nothing is sent to the agent; the next request you make carries the style.

## The file

```markdown
---
name: gym-vlog
title: Gym vlog
description: Fast, loud and bright.
avg_shot: 1.6
cut_on_beat: 0.5
transitions: [cut, whip 0.2]
captions: Big Impact, pop, 2 words per cue
loudness: -14
grade: punchy, warm
pacing: [1.4, 1.6, 1.8, 1.5]
words_per_minute: 180
---
Open on the heaviest lift. Cut on the beat in the training montage.
```

`avg_shot` is in seconds; `cut_on_beat` is the share of cuts on a beat (0 to 1, or a percentage);
`transitions` lists kinds with a typical length in seconds; `pacing` is the average shot length in each
quarter of the video. Every field is optional, and keys the app does not know are kept as they are.

## Learn this style

**Learn this style…** in the library measures a reference video and writes a draft style for you to
review. The video is only read, never changed.

![Learn a style, with a draft](/img/tutorial/learn-style.png)

1. **Choose…** the video and give the draft a name.
2. Measured every time: the cuts (hard cuts, dissolves and dips to black), every shot's length, the average
   and the pacing by quarter, the share of cuts on the music's beat (when it has a steady one), the
   loudness, and the colour of each shot (level, contrast, saturation, warm or cool). **Words per minute**
   needs speech-to-text, which **Transcript ▸ Get captions** installs.
3. **Describe** (optional): the video describer, an add-on, looks at three frames of each shot and writes its
   framing (close-up, medium, wide), subject, camera move and any text on screen. The first time, the
   window offers to install it: **SmolVLM2 256M** (about 300 MB), or the larger **SmolVLM2 2.2B** (about
   1.7 GB, better and slower), running on this computer through llama.cpp. **Measure only** skips it. Long
   videos are sampled: at most the number of shots you set are described.
4. Click **Learn**. The draft opens when it is done: the rules, a summary, and a table of every shot. Edit
   anything, then **Save to styles** or **Save and apply**.

The describer only describes: every number in a learned style comes from the measurements.

## For agents (MCP)

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| `styles.list` / `styles.get` | the library; one style's rules, body and file text |
| `styles.save` / `styles.delete` | write or remove one of the user's styles (asks the user first) |
| `styles.apply` / `styles.remove` | set the project's style / take it out (one undo step) |
| `styles.learn` + `styles.learn_status` | learn from a video in the background; the status carries the draft |
| `styles.describer` / `styles.install_describer` | the describer add-on's state / install it (a download: asks first) |

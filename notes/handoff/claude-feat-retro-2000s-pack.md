# retro-pack: `claude/feat-retro-2000s-pack`

Classic 2000s pack: our own remakes of the early-2000s home-movie editor look. No Microsoft names, assets
or logos anywhere (the app text says "Classic 2000s"). Branched from `origin/develop` at `41c9b73`.

## Done

- **Transitions (26)**, all in `library/transitions`, tagged `classic 2000s`:
  - 18 new `classic-*.glsl`: Pixel Dissolve, Fade Through Black, Fade Through White, Bars,
    Checkerboard, Diagonal Wipe, Heart, Keyhole, Eye Iris, Rectangle Zoom, Flip, Roll, Shatter In,
    Sweep, Whirlwind, Spiral, Zig-Zag, Bow Tie.
  - 8 existing ones that already fit, retagged: blinds, clock-wipe, iris-circle / diamond / star,
    page-turn (the page curl), pixelate, ripple.
  - Fade in/out from black or white = Fade Through Black/White on a clip edge (library transitions
    already apply at an edge).
- **Effects (10)**:
  - 8 new `library/effects/classic-*.glsl`: Sepia Tone, Black and White, Soft Blur, Posterize,
    Watercolour, 3D Ripple, Edge Detect, Film Age.
  - Retagged: old-film, mirror.
  - Speed Up (2x, 4x) and Slow Down (1/2, 1/4) are constant speed curves added to
    `presets::builtin_speed_ramps()` (Gallery ▸ Speed).
- **`src/engine/retro.rs`**: the pack registry.
  - Every pack shader is `include_str!`'d, so the slideshow and the Gallery work offline.
    `ensure_installed()` (called in `App::new` on a background thread) copies them into
    `%APPDATA%\SimpleEditor\library` once per `PACK_VERSION`, writing a `.classic-2000s-1` marker.
    It never overwrites a user's file and doesn't re-add one the user removed.
  - `library::merge` names, describes and tags installed pack items from their own header when no
    catalogue is cached. Otherwise they would show as untagged "yours".
- **Titles (11)**, native text and shape clips added to `builtin_titles()` under a third Titles
  category, "Classic 2000s":
  - Classic Blue Title (the royal-blue card, white text, drop shadow), Basic (drop shadow), Fade,
    Fly-In, Typewriter, News Banner, Zoom, Spin, Moving, Credits Roll (Directed by / Starring /
    Produced by / Music / Thanks for watching) and The End.
  - Each sets a `thumb_t`, so its Gallery picture shows the text rather than an empty blue card.
- **Font**: `retro::FONT = "Comic Sans MS, Comic Neue"`. That relies on the existing comma fallback in
  `text.rs::font_for`, which is now split into `face_for` plus `resolved_family`.
  - Comic Sans is never bundled.
  - Comic Neue Bold (SIL OFL, `assets/fonts/OFL-ComicNeue.txt`) is a Latin subset of 39,860 B, added
    to `BUNDLED_FONTS`.
- **Make Slideshow** (`src/engine/slideshow.rs`):
  - Photos and clips go in order on one video track. Each photo gets an alternating in/out Ken Burns
    block. Each cut gets a classic shader transition (seeded random with no repeats in a row, a chosen
    one, or none).
  - A blue title card opens it and a scrolling credits card closes it, with the text on a track above
    the card.
  - Optional music runs from the start, is cut at the end and fades out. `fit_to_music` shares what
    the cards and clips leave of the song among the photos, with a minimum of 1 s each (`fits: false`
    when the song is too short).
  - `timing()` is a separate pure function.
- **UI and MCP** (`src/ui/app/retro_ctl.rs`):
  - `slideshow.make` is a Mutate tool, so it is one undo step. Its default items are the Library
    multi-selection, else the timeline selection's media, else the Library's current item.
  - `slideshow.transitions` is a Read tool.
  - A **Make Slideshow** card in Gallery ▸ Titles ▸ Classic 2000s (drawn picture, no drag) runs the
    tool and shows a toast.
  - The Effects and Transitions tabs gain a fixed "Classic 2000s" category. It matches the tag
    case-insensitively.
- **Docs**: CHANGELOG under `## Unreleased`, `website/docs/classic-2000s.mdx` (added to the sidebar),
  and two rows in `docs/customizing.md`.
- **index.json and thumbnails**:
  - `index.json` was regenerated by a Python port of `entry_of`. Run against develop's own library,
    the port reproduces develop's `index.json` byte for byte.
  - The 26 new thumbnails were rendered headlessly with Mesa from a port of the GL test's stand-in
    pictures. The port re-renders the existing Blinds thumbnail to match the committed one.

  already on develop.
- Every new or changed shader passes `glslangValidator` when wrapped in the app's real `PRELUDE`,
  `MAIN` and transition wrapper (extracted from `shaders.rs`).

  - progress 0 is exactly the outgoing picture and progress 1 exactly the incoming one (max

  - Heart edge and growth;

## Risks / notes

- **Title layout**: titles are laid out on a 1920x1080 frame (cards are 1920x1080 shapes, News Banner
  offsets are in px), the same as the existing built-in titles. In a 4K or vertical project the blue
  card won't fill the frame. The slideshow uses the project's real size.
- **Image fit**: Make Slideshow relies on image clips being fitted to the frame by the compositor.
  Ken Burns scales 1.0 to 1.15, so a photo of another aspect ratio keeps its letterbox bars.
- **Tool names**: parity-editing also plans a "slideshow/montage generator". If it lands, it should
  reuse `engine::slideshow` and `slideshow.make` rather than add a second one.
- **Thumbnails offline**: pack items installed offline show thumbnails only once the online library
  has fetched them, which is the same as any installed library item.

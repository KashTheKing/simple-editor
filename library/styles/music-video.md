The song is the editor: every cut lands on the music.

- Cut every 2 beats; double the rate in the loudest sections (chorus, drop), halve it in quiet intros and breakdowns.
- Cuts sit one frame before the beat so they feel on it.
- Use the moving part of each clip; never the same clip twice in a row; save the best shots for the chorus.
- Accent the strongest hits with a zoom punch or a 0.15 s flash; a speed ramp on about 1 shot in 8.
- About 80% hard cuts, the rest flash, whip or glitch.
- One look for the whole video.

Run `/style-music-video` to have the assistant cut it.

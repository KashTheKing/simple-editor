Each clip is one complete idea - a claim, a story or an answer with a payoff.

- 30-60 s for vertical, 60-120 s for horizontal.
- Start on a strong first sentence or a 2-3 s title with the question being answered; no "so, um, yeah".
- Tight but natural pauses; speaker changes keep 0.4-0.6 s.
- The active speaker is on screen; with two cameras, cut to whoever speaks.
- Voices cleaned and matched; -16 LUFS for podcast feeds, -14 for social.

Run `/style-podcast-clips` to have the assistant find and cut the clips.

Built for the swipe: the first second decides everything.

- The strongest line or image in the first 1-2 s, with a 2.5 s hook text over it.
- A visual change every 1.5-3 s: a cut, a punch-in, a cutaway or text.
- Pauses cut tight (about 0.25 s).
- Punch in on every second sentence.
- Nothing important in the top 15%, the bottom 35% or the right-hand button rail.
- End on the payoff, no outro card; a loopable last frame is a bonus.

Patient and trustworthy: the story comes from the people in it and the pictures prove what they say.

- Only dead air goes; thoughtful pauses stay.
- Average shot 5-15 s, varied, never uniform.
- B-roll over 40-60% of interview time, each 4-8 s, entering on the noun being described.
- L-cuts out of the interview into B-roll, J-cuts into a new speaker or place (0.5-1 s).
- Hard cuts almost everywhere; a 0.5-1 s dissolve only for a change of time or place.
- Dialogue first; ambience under it; music only between lines, ducked about 18 dB.

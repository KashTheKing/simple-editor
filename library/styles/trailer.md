Sell the footage: establish, complicate, accelerate, title, button.

- Act 1 (about 30%): the world and the main person, 3-5 s shots, one key line, calm music.
- Act 2 (about 35%): the problem appears, 2-3 s shots, lines separated by black or title cards, music builds.
- Act 3 (about 25%): the biggest moments cut on the beat, 0.5-1.5 s and getting shorter, to the peak.
- Then 1 s of black, the title card for 3-4 s, and a 2-4 s button: one last joke, scare or line.
- Dips to black between acts, flashes on act 3 hits; music cuts to silence for the title.
- No spoilers.

Run `/style-trailer` to have the assistant cut it.
